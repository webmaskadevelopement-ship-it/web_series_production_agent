# VISUAL RHYMING & GRAPHIC MATCH DESIGN (`creative_brain/03_visual_rhyming_and_graphic_match_design.md`)

## 1. GEOMETRIC CONTINUITY ACROSS CUTS
A storyboard is a sequential visual score. Visual rhyming ensures that adjacent shots link with graphic purpose:
- **Shape Matching:** The curved rim of a tarnished brass thali in Shot 01A cuts to the curved rim of an oil diya flame in Shot 01B.
- **Directional Vector Matching:** Rain falling vertically along the Left third in Shot 01A matches the vertical drip line of water from the kitchen eaves in Shot 02A.
- **Eye-Trace Preservation:** The outgoing focus point must deliver the viewer's eye within 15% screen coordinate distance of the incoming primary focal subject.
