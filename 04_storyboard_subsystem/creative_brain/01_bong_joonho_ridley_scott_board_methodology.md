# BONG JOON-HO & RIDLEY SCOTT STORYBOARD METHODOLOGY (`creative_brain/01_bong_joonho_ridley_scott_board_methodology.md`)

## 1. THE EDITORIAL BLUEPRINT IN DRAWN FORM
Bong Joon-ho (*Parasite*, *Memories of Murder*) and Ridley Scott (*Alien*, *Blade Runner*) famously storyboard every single shot before setting foot on set. Bong shoots zero extraneous coverage; his storyboard is the literal edit of the film.

In this methodology:
1. **Graphic Juxtaposition:** The relationship between outgoing Panel $N$ and incoming Panel $N+1$ must create visual contrast or graphic harmony.
2. **Camera Blocking Drawn into the Frame:** Directional 3D arrows, depth planes, and lens markers are embedded directly on the panel card.
3. **No Decorative Panels:** Every panel must represent an actual cut, framing decision, or camera displacement.
