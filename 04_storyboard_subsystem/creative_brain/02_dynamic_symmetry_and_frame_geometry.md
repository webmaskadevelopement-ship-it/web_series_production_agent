# DYNAMIC SYMMETRY & FRAME GEOMETRY (`creative_brain/02_dynamic_symmetry_and_frame_geometry.md`)

## 1. CLASSICAL COMPOSITION IN 2.39:1 ANAMORPHIC WIDESCREEN
In wide cinematic aspect ratios (2.39:1 / 21:9), the Rule of Thirds is often insufficient. Sub-System 04 applies classical **Dynamic Symmetry**:
- **Baroque Diagonal:** Bottom-Left to Top-Right. Conveys rising tension, hope, or forward striving.
- **Sinister Diagonal:** Top-Left to Bottom-Right. Conveys decline, descent, moral collapse, or inevitable entrapment.
- **Root-4 & Root-5 Rectangles:** Slicing the wide canvas into harmonic quadrants where architectural verticals (such as haveli stone pillars) split the frame precisely at $48\%-54\%$ X-axis coordinates.
