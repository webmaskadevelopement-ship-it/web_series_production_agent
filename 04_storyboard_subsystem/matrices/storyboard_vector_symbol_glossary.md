# STORYBOARD VECTOR SYMBOL GLOSSARY (`matrices/storyboard_vector_symbol_glossary.md`)

## 1. VECTOR NOTATION DIRECTORY

- `[CAM_PUSH_Z+]`: Camera forward push along Z axis into the set.
- `[CAM_PULL_Z-]`: Camera tracking back out of the set.
- `[CAM_PAN_X+]`: Camera panning horizontally to the right.
- `[CAM_PAN_X-]`: Camera panning horizontally to the left.
- `[CAM_PED_Y+]`: Camera ped/boom rising vertically.
- `[CAM_PED_Y-]`: Camera ped/boom sinking vertically.
- `[RACK_FG_TO_MG]`: Focus pulling from Foreground plane to Midground plane.
- `[RACK_MG_TO_BG]`: Focus pulling from Midground plane to Background plane.
- `[SUBJ_CROSS_X+]`: Actor walking across frame from left to right.
- `[SUBJ_APPROACH_Z-]`: Actor walking downstage toward camera.
- `[ENV_RAIN_Y-]`: Rain/water droplets falling vertically downward.
