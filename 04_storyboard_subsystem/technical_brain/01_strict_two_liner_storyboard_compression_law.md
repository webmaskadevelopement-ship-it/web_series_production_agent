# STRICT TWO-LINER STORYBOARD COMPRESSION LAW (`technical_brain/01_strict_two_liner_storyboard_compression_law.md`)

## 1. THE TWO-LINER RULE
To eliminate bloated prose and force concise, graphic visual direction, every storyboard card in `04_Storyboarding_File.md` must enforce the **Strict 2-Liner Visual Description Rule**:

### Line 1: Spatial Composition & Staging Geometry
- Must state the exact camera lens, aspect ratio, primary architectural division, and character positions across screen quadrants (Left, Center, Right) and Z-depth.
- *Example:*
  > **Line 01:** A central weathered sandstone pillar slices the 2.39:1 frame vertically in half—isolating Raghav frozen in the dark rain-streaked archway on the Left third (Z:6.1m) from Savitri seated on the floor in a warm pool of oil-lamp light on the Right third (Z:4.2m).

### Line 2: Kinetic Action & Focal Shift
- Must describe the camera displacement, rack focus timing, character micro-movement, and environmental particle behavior.
- *Example:*
  > **Line 02:** As the camera creeps slowly forward along the wet courtyard axis, focus racks from the blurred foreground brass water pot to Savitri's unhurried hands peeling garlic while rainwater drips rhythmically from Raghav's umbrella tip.
