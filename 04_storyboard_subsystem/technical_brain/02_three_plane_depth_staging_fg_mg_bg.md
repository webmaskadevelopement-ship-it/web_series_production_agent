# THREE-PLANE DEPTH STAGING (FG, MG, BG) (`technical_brain/02_three_plane_depth_staging_fg_mg_bg.md`)

## 1. Z-DEPTH CODIFICATION
Every storyboard panel card must deconstruct the composition into three distinct optical planes:

1. **Foreground Plane (FG) [0.5m – 1.8m]:**
   - Out-of-focus framing elements, doorframes, hanging bells, or brass Kalash creating depth occlusion and dirty framing.
2. **Midground Plane (MG) [2.0m – 6.5m]:**
   - The primary dramatic action zone where subjects perform, speak, and interact with hero props.
3. **Background Plane (BG) [7.0m – 15.0m+]:**
   - Architectural context, deep dark arched corridors, secondary light sources (caged 2700K bulbs), and falling rain beds that establish spatial scale.
