# THREE-VALUE TONAL SKETCH (KEY, MID, SHADOW) (`technical_brain/04_three_value_tonal_sketch_key_mid_shadow.md`)

## 1. THREE-VALUE TONAL ALLOCATION
Master storyboard artists reduce complex lighting into three distinct graphic value buckets:

1. **Shadow Value ($4-15\text{ IRE}$):** Represents dark architectural stone walls, night corridors, and negative fill zones. Occupies $65\%-75\%$ of frame area in chiaroscuro dusk scenes.
2. **Midtone Value ($35-48\text{ IRE}$):** Represents ambient twilight sky reflection, damp floor sandstone, and character skin midtones. Occupies $20\%-25\%$ of frame.
3. **Highlight Value ($85-98\text{ IRE}$):** Represents practical oil diya core, brass thali specular reflection, and umbrella water droplets. Occupies $5\%-10\%$ of frame.
