# WALTER MURCH CLEAR DENSITY & METAPHOR (`creative_brain/01_walter_murch_clear_density_and_metaphor.md`)

## 1. THE PRINCIPLE OF "CLEAR DENSITY"
Walter Murch (*Apocalypse Now*, *The Godfather*, *The English Patient*) established that human hearing can only consciously track a maximum of **two and a half sonic elements** at any single moment. Stacking 20 dense sound effects simultaneously results in acoustic sludge.

"Clear Density" demands:
1. **Ruthless Hierarchy:** In any given frame, one element leads (e.g., the fibrous crack of garlic skin), one element supports (a distant muffled temple bell), and one subtle ambient layer anchors (dusk courtyard humidity air bed). Everything else is carved out.
2. **Metaphorical Sound:** Sounds must carry emotional narrative meaning. E.g., The heavy rainwater drops detaching from Raghav's umbrella and striking sandstone are not just water—they sound like an unyielding acoustic clock ticking away his remaining time with his mother.
