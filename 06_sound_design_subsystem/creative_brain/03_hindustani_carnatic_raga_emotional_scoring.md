## 1. RAGA TIME THEORY & MICROTONAL TENSION
In classical Indian musical theory, ragas are aligned with specific periods of the day (*Prahar*) and emotional states (*Rasas*):

| Time / Prahar | Classical Raga | Scale / Note Architecture | Dominant Rasa & Acoustic Character |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dusk (Sandhi-Prakash)** | **Raga Marwa** | Sa, Komal Re, Ga, Tivra Ma, Dha, Ni (Omits Pancham / 5th) | *Karuna* (Pathos / Unease). Denies harmonic resolution; the omission of the 5th creates perpetual psychological yearning. |
| **Late Night (Nishitha)** | **Raga Malkauns** | Sa, Komal Ga, Shuddha Ma, Komal Dha, Komal Ni | *Shanta / Bhayanaka* (Meditative solitude, ancient subterranean power). |
| **Early Morning (Bhor)** | **Raga Bhairav** | Sa, Komal Re, Ga, Ma, Pa, Komal Dha, Ni | *Veera / Karuna* (Solemn spiritual awakening, moral confrontation). |
| **Monsoon / Rain (Megh)** | **Raga Megh Malhar** | Sa, Re, Ma, Pa, Komal Ni | *Shringara / Karuna* (Rain atmosphere, thunderous low strings). |
