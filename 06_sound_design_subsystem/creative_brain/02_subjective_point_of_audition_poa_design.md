# SUBJECTIVE POINT-OF-AUDITION (POA) DESIGN (`creative_brain/02_subjective_point_of_audition_poa_design.md`)

## 1. OBJECTIVE ROOM SOUND VS. SUBJECTIVE PSYCHOLOGICAL AUDITION
In Sub-System 06, sound design shifts dynamically between:
- **Objective Acoustic Reality:** What a microphone placed in the room physically records (e.g., standard reverberation off haveli stone pillars, rain falling through the courtyard).
- **Subjective Point-of-Audition (POA):** What a character psychologically hears under acute emotional trauma:
  - When guilt overwhelms Raghav, the ambient rain bed and courtyard acoustics dynamically low-pass filter at $800\text{ Hz}$, while his own internal heartbeat and a sub-bass $34.6\text{ Hz}$ LFE pulse swell by $+6\text{ dB}$.
  - The sound mixer pulls the viewer directly into the character's internal claustrophobia.
