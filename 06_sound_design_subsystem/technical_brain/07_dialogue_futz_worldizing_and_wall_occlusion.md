# DIALOGUE FUTZ, WORLDIZING & WALL OCCLUSION (`technical_brain/07_dialogue_futz_worldizing_and_wall_occlusion.md`)

## 1. ACOUSTIC OCCLUSION & WORLDIZING
When dialogue or spot sound occurs behind architectural barriers (e.g., Savitri speaking from the veranda while the camera is outside in the alley):
- **Acoustic Occlusion Low-Pass Filter:** $60\text{cm}$ thick load-bearing Lakhori brick walls filter all frequencies above $1.4\text{ kHz}$ at $18\text{ dB/octave}$.
- **Worldizing IR:** The dialogue track is routed through Altiverb *Behind Thick Stone Wall* impulse response, pulling direct sound down by $-12\text{ dB}$ while boosting early wall slap reflections.
