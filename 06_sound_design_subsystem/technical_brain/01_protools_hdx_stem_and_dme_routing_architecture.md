# PRO TOOLS HDX STEM & DME ROUTING ARCHITECTURE (`technical_brain/01_protools_hdx_stem_and_dme_routing_architecture.md`)

## 1. 7-STEM DM&E ROUTING BUS SPECIFICATION
The sound design delivery engine structures audio into 7 dedicated Pro Tools HDX routing folder stems:

1. **STEM_01_fDx (Dialogue Folder):** 8 Production Dx tracks + 4 ADR tracks + 2 Dx Futz/Worldize tracks. Direct to Center/Left/Right.
2. **STEM_02_fVO (Voiceover Master Bus):** Center channel object with sidechain ducking sends routed to Mx and BG stems.
3. **STEM_03_fFoley (Foley Folder):** Cloth Mono, Footsteps L/R, Specific Props Mono/Stereo.
4. **STEM_04_fFxA (Hard Diegetic Spot Effects):** Water drips, door slams, iron latch transients, rain impacts.
5. **STEM_05_fFxB (Designed Psychological Sound Effects):** Sub-bass LFE tension drones, risers, time-stretched metal resonance.
6. **STEM_06_fBG (Backgrounds & Ambiences):** Quad surround beds (L/R/Ls/Rs) + 4 Top Height air beds (Ltf/Rtf/Ltr/Rtr).
7. **STEM_07_fMx (Score & Music Bus):** Acoustic raga score and traditional instrument beds.
