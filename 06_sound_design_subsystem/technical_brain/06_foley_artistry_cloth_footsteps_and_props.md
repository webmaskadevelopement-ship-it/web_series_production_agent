# FOLEY ARTISTRY: CLOTH, FOOTSTEPS & PROPS (`technical_brain/06_foley_artistry_cloth_footsteps_and_props.md`)

## 1. FOLEY RECORDING SPECIFICATIONS
Foley cues must be recorded and synchronized to picture frames:
- **Microphone Choice:** Sennheiser MKH 8040 hyper-cardioid at $10\text{ cm} – 15\text{ cm}$ in a dead acoustic pit; transient boosted $+3\text{ dB}$ at $3.2\text{ kHz}$ for tactile clarity.
- **Surface Materials:** Wet unpolished Chunar sandstone, dry coir mat, aged Burma teak floorboards.
- **Sync Frame Logging:** Every Foley event must log its exact transient picture frame (e.g., *Garlic skin crack: Frame 26, Frame 88, Frame 134*).
- **Cloth & Movement:** Wet linen sleeve friction (`-31.0 dBFS`), soaked trouser hem mud slap (`-26.0 dBFS`).
