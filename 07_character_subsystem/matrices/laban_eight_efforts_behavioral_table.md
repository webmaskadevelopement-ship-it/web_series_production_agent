# LABAN EIGHT EFFORTS BEHAVIORAL TABLE (`matrices/laban_eight_efforts_behavioral_table.md`)

## 1. THE EIGHT BASIC EFFORT ACTIONS

| Effort Action | Space | Weight | Time | Flow | Dramatic Psychological Correlate |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Press** | Direct | Heavy | Sustained | Bound | Crushing pressure, authoritative intimidation, suppressed grief. |
| **Wring** | Indirect | Heavy | Sustained | Bound | Guilt entrapment, internal psychological agony, inability to flee. |
| **Glide** | Direct | Light | Sustained | Free | Calm domestic routine, methodical detachment, steady control. |
| **Float** | Indirect | Light | Sustained | Free | Disassociation, dreamlike grief, wandering memory. |
| **Punch / Thrust**| Direct | Heavy | Sudden | Bound | Sudden explosive rage, slamming an object, violent accusation. |
| **Slash** | Indirect | Heavy | Sudden | Free | Wild panicked thrashing, physical collapse. |
| **Dab** | Direct | Light | Sudden | Bound | Tentative touching of a taboo object, hesitant inquiry. |
| **Flick** | Indirect | Light | Sudden | Free | Casual dismissal, contemptuous waving away of an argument. |
