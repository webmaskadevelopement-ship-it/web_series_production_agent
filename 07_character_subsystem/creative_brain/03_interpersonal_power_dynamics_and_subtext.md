# INTERPERSONAL POWER DYNAMICS & SUBTEXT (`creative_brain/03_interpersonal_power_dynamics_and_subtext.md`)

## 1. STATUS TRANSACTIONS (KEITH JOHNSTONE APPLIED)
Dramatic tension is governed by the shifting see-saw of character status:
- **Raghav’s High-Status Pretense:** Walks in standing tall, wearing expensive tailored clothes, carrying legal documents, speaking formal English/Khari Boli nouns.
- **Savitri’s Low-Status Domination:** Seated low on the floor, barefoot, handling garlic, never looking up. By refusing to look at him or grant him a greeting, she holds absolute moral high status, forcing Raghav to fidget, stutter, and collapse down to childhood subordination.
