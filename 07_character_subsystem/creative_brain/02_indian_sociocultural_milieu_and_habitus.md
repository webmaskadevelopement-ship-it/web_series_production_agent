## 1. BOURDIEU’S HABITUS IN THE INDIAN CONTEXT
Habitus represents the deeply ingrained social habits, postures, bodily reflexes, and linguistic mannerisms acquired through class and caste upbringing:
- **First-Generation Metropolitan Migrant (Raghav):** Caught between Lutyens' Delhi corporate efficiency and Purvanchali domestic orthodoxy. Checks a titanium wristwatch obsessively; cannot walk on wet mossy stones without slipping in urban leather soles.
- **The Orthodox Domestic Custodian (Savitri):** Bodily habitus rooted in 40 years of domestic ritual. Sits flat on the floor with folded legs (*Chauki*); handling brass vessels with effortless rhythmic economy. Refuses to acknowledge modern legal paperwork.
