# PSYCHOLOGICAL WOUND, LIE, NEED & DESIRE MATRIX (`creative_brain/01_psychological_wound_lie_need_desire_matrix.md`)

## 1. THE FOUR-PILLAR CHARACTER ENGINE
Every character in Sub-System 07 is built upon four psychological pillars:

1. **The Core Wound (*The Ghost*):** An unhealed formative trauma from the past that shapes present defense mechanisms (e.g., Raghav refusing to return from Delhi for his father's final week of paralysis to attend a High Court interview).
2. **The Internal Lie:** The cognitive distortion the character tells themselves to justify their avoidance of pain (e.g., believing that moving his mother to an air-conditioned Noida flat and paying off debts will erase moral abandonment).
3. **The Conscious Outer Want:** The immediate, tangible goal driving their scene actions (e.g., obtaining Savitri's thumbprint/signature on the sale deed before the Monday morning train).
4. **The Unconscious Inner Need:** The painful spiritual or psychological surrender required for true healing (e.g., weeping at his father's empty cot and submitting to his mother's moral judgment).
