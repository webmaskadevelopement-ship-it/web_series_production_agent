# RUDOLF LABAN BESS MOVEMENT & KINESPHERE (`technical_brain/02_rudolf_laban_bess_movement_and_kinesphere.md`)

## 1. LABAN MOVEMENT ANALYSIS (BODY, EFFORT, SHAPE, SPACE)
Sub-System 07 defines actor physical performance through Rudolf Laban's Effort taxonomy:

### The Four Motion Factors
1. **Space:** Direct (laser focused) vs. Indirect (wandering, exploratory).
2. **Weight:** Heavy (grounded, forceful, burdened) vs. Light (delicate, tentative).
3. **Time:** Sudden (urgent, reactive) vs. Sustained (lingering, unhurried).
4. **Flow:** Bound (restrained, contracted, rigid) vs. Free (fluid, released).

### Character Profiles
- **Raghav Sharma:** Operates in a **Press** drive (Direct + Heavy + Sustained + Bound Flow) when exerting legal dominance; shifts to **Wring** (Indirect + Heavy + Sustained + Bound Flow) when trapped by ancestral guilt. Narrow near-reach kinesphere (elbows pinned to ribs).
- **Savitri:** Operates in a **Glide / Press** hybrid (Direct + Light + Sustained + Bound Flow) during domestic garlic peeling, masking volcanic internal resistance.
