# PAUL EKMAN FACS ACTION UNIT MICRO-EXPRESSIONS (`technical_brain/01_paul_ekman_facs_action_unit_micro_expressions.md`)

## 1. FACIAL ACTION CODING SYSTEM (FACS)
Sub-System 07 encodes character facial expressions via Paul Ekman's anatomical Action Units (AUs):

| Action Unit (AU) | Muscular Basis | Behavioral Appearance | Dramatic Emotion |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **AU1 + AU4** | Frontalis (pars medialis) + Corrugator supercilii | Oblique grief triangle; inner brow pulled up and together | Suppressed guilt, filial sorrow |
| **AU4** | Corrugator supercilii, Depressor supercilii | Brows drawn down and together | Suppressed irritation, moral defiance |
| **AU7** | Orbicularis oculi (pars palpebralis) | Lower eyelid tightened / narrowed | Suspicion, scrutinizing a lie |
| **AU12** | Zygomaticus major | Lip corners pulled obliquely upward | Social politeness, mask smile |
| **AU15** | Depressor anguli oris | Lip corners depressed downward | Bitter grief, disappointment |
| **AU17** | Mentalis | Chin boss raised and wrinkled | Rejection, refusal to weep |
| **AU23 + AU24** | Orbicularis oris | Lips tightened and pressed firmly together | Suppressed rage, refusal to speak |
| **AU31** | Masseter clench | Jaw muscles bulge visibly under skin | Suppressed aggression, nervous strain |
| **AU54** | Neck flexors | Head tilted downward $8^\circ-15^\circ$ | Shame, submission, guilt |
| **AU61 + AU64** | Inferior rectus | Eye gaze averted downward and to the left | Deceit, inability to hold eye contact |
