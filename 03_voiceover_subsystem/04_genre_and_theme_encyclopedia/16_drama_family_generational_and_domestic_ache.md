# GENRE PRODUCTION PLAYBOOK: DRAMA: FAMILY GENERATIONAL & DOMESTIC ACHE (`16_drama_family_generational_and_domestic_ache.md`)
*(Unspoken Middle-Class Sacrifices, Courtyard Silences & Inheritance Wounds)*

## 1. GENRE PHILOSOPHY & NARRATIVE CONTRACT
In family and generational drama (Kapoor & Sons, Piku, Monsoon Wedding), tragedy lives in the quiet domestic corners: the property dispute between brothers who haven't spoken in ten years, the mother packing tiffin boxes while hiding her illness, the daughter carrying the burden of family honor, and the dining table where spoons clink in heavy, suffocating silence.

---

## 2. RECOMMENDED WRITER BRAIN BLEND
- **Munshi Premchand (40%):** Moral weight, economic arithmetic, quiet human dignity.
- **Gulzar (35%):** Domestic sensory nostalgia, personified domestic objects, tactile silence.
- **Varun Grover (25%):** Modern generational friction, unsentimental observation.

---

## 3. ETYMOLOGICAL & DIALECT REGISTER SPEC
- **Register:** Warm Everyday Hindustani `55%` + Domestic Tadbhava `35%` + Middle-Class English Slang `10%`.
- **Vocabulary:** आंगन, चूल्हा, दालान, वसीयत, बीमारी, दवाई, इतवार, शादी, बेटी, कर्ज़, सन्नाटा, बाबूजी.
- **Banned:** Theatrical Bollywood mother weeping melodrama, screaming over-acting.

---

## 4. SYNTACTIC PACING & WPM ENVELOPE
- **Pacing:** 82–95 WPM. Gentle, conversational, deeply humane.
- **Average Sentence Length:** 8–12 words.
- **Pause Structure:** Loaded 1.0s silences after domestic micro-observations.

---

## 5. ACOUSTIC & VOCAL TIMBRE DIRECTIVE
- **Vocal Timbre:** Warm, vulnerable, weathered baritone/alto (F0: 95–110 Hz).
- **Mic Placement:** Large diaphragm condenser at 10 cm, intimate conversational presence.
- **DSP Chain:** Altiverb 'Domestic Living Room' (4% wet), gentle optical compression.

## 7. 5 STRUCTURAL SCENE ARCHETYPES FOR THIS GENRE
1. **The Shared Morning Routine:** The parents waking up before dawn, moving quietly to not disturb children.
2. **The Financial Ledger:** The father and mother reviewing monthly bills under a single lamp.
3. **The Property Dispute:** Brothers dividing the ancestral rooms with chalk lines on the floor.
4. **The Departure of the Child:** Packing a trunk with homemade snacks for a distant metro city.
5. **The Final Empty House:** The parents sitting alone on the veranda as evening settles.

---

## 8. CONTRAPUNTAL FRAME-TO-VOICE RULES
- Over family members shouting at each other on screen, VO speaks of the photograph of them as children.
- Never describe the tears; describe the unfinished food left on the plate.
- Ground all generational friction in tangible physical artifacts (pension passbooks, medicine bottles).

---

## 9. GENRE CLICHÉ TRAPS TO AVOID
- ❌ Melodramatic screaming and soap-opera background violins.
- ❌ Villainizing one family member completely; all are trapped by circumstance.
- ❌ Easy, magical happy reconciliations where all trauma evaporates.

---

## 10. 3 FULL PRODUCTION-GRADE SAMPLE VO CUES
### Cue 1: The Partition of the Home
- **Devanagari:** उस आंगन के बीचों-बीच... चूने की एक सफेद लकीर खींच दी गई। [PAUSE: 0.8s] जिस जमीन पर दोनों भाई बचपन में घुटनों के बल चले थे, आज उसी जमीन पर दो अलग-अलग चूल्हे जलने वाले थे। मां कमरे के भीतर बैठी रही... उसने बाहर आकर लकीर नहीं देखी, क्योंकि वह जानती थी कि यह लकीर जमीन पर नहीं, उसकी छाती पर खींची गई है।
- **Subtext:** The devastating, quiet reality of property partition between siblings.

### Cue 2: The Father's Silent Illness
- **Devanagari:** बाबूजी ने कभी नहीं बताया कि उनके सीने में दर्द कब से है। [PAUSE: 0.6s] बस हर इतवार को अलमारी के सबसे नीचे वाले दराज में अपनी दवा की शीशियां छिपाकर रख देते थे... ताकि बच्चे यह न सोचें कि उनकी पढ़ाई के पैसों से बाप अपनी सांसे खरीद रहा है। यही था उनका संसार... चुपचाप सहना और चुपचाप मुस्कुराना।
- **Subtext:** The quiet, tragic martyrdom of Indian middle-class fathers.

### Cue 3: The Empty Nest
- **Devanagari:** बच्चे शहर चले गए थे। [PAUSE: 1.0s] घर में अब सिर्फ़ दो बूढ़े लोग थे, और वह पुराना टीवी जो दिन भर बिना किसी आवाज़ के चलता रहता था... सिर्फ़ इसलिए ताकि घर में ऐसा लगे कि कोई जीवित है। शाम ढली, चौखट पर दीया जला... और दोनों ने एक-दूसरे को देखे बिना अपनी-अपनी थाली खिसका ली।
- **Subtext:** The heavy, crushing quietude of aging parents left behind.
