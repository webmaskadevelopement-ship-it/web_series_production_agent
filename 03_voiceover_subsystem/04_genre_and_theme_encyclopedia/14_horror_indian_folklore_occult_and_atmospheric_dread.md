# GENRE PRODUCTION PLAYBOOK: HORROR: INDIAN FOLKLORE, OCCULT & ATMOSPHERIC DREAD (`14_horror_indian_folklore_occult_and_atmospheric_dread.md`)
*(Tumbbad-Style Ancestral Curse, Uncanny Nature & Greed-as-Monster Dread)*

## 1. GENRE PHILOSOPHY & NARRATIVE CONTRACT
In Indian folklore and atmospheric dread horror (Tumbbad, Pari, Bramayugam), horror does not come from American jump scares; it oozes from the damp soil, ancestral mansions rotting in perpetual monsoon rain, forbidden village fables, grandmother's warnings, and the bottomless, terrifying abyss of human greed waking ancient, cursed deities.

---

## 2. RECOMMENDED WRITER BRAIN BLEND
- **Phanishwar Nath Renu (40%):** Soil texture, regional folk superstition, rural acoustic dread.
- **Gulzar (35%):** Tactile dampness, rotting wood, sensory decay, unraveled time.
- **Saadat Hasan Manto (25%):** The repulsive biological reality of greed and flesh.

---

## 3. ETYMOLOGICAL & DIALECT REGISTER SPEC
- **Register:** Anchalik Folk Hindi `50%` + Archaic Mythic Roots `35%` + Whispered Deshaj `15%`.
- **Vocabulary:** श्राप, हवेली, दलदल, लालच, खजाना, सड़ांध, पुरखे, कोख, अंधकार, दीया, राक्षस, मुक्ति.
- **Banned:** Hollywood slasher tropes, modern teenagers camping, cheap sound effects.

---

## 4. SYNTACTIC PACING & WPM ENVELOPE
- **Pacing:** 75–88 WPM. Hushed, deliberate, bedtime-fable cadence.
- **Average Sentence Length:** 7–10 words.
- **Pause Structure:** Long 1.4s void-holds letting the ambient dripping rain and floorboard creaks breathe.

---

## 5. ACOUSTIC & VOCAL TIMBRE DIRECTIVE
- **Vocal Timbre:** Hushed, resonant low baritone (F0: 82–96 Hz). Whispered vocal fry.
- **Mic Placement:** Large diaphragm condenser at 8 cm, extreme intimate proximity.
- **DSP Chain:** Altiverb 'Damp Stone Cellar' (8% wet), high-shelf roll-off at 7 kHz for dark intimacy.

## 7. 5 STRUCTURAL SCENE ARCHETYPES FOR THIS GENRE
1. **The Ancestral Fable:** Opening with a bedtime warning from a grandmother that was ignored.
2. **The Descent into the Cellar:** Entering the subterranean womb where the ancient entity feeds.
3. **The Gold Temptation:** Seeing the impossible wealth that demands human blood in trade.
4. **The Monstrous Transformation:** Watching a family elder mutate into an insatiable rotting ghoul.
5. **The Trapped Heir:** The vault door sealing shut from outside as the monster awakens.

---

## 8. CONTRAPUNTAL FRAME-TO-VOICE RULES
- Never raise your voice; speak like someone terrified of waking the entity in the next room.
- Contrast the glittering allure of gold with the sickening physical texture of rotten flour and meat.
- Let the persistent dripping of monsoon water carry the inescapable rhythm of the curse.

---

## 9. GENRE CLICHÉ TRAPS TO AVOID
- ❌ Cheap Western jump scares with loud synthesizers.
- ❌ Scientific explanations debunking the ancient curse.
- ❌ Treating horror as funny; maintain heavy, ritualistic dread.

---

## 10. 3 FULL PRODUCTION-GRADE SAMPLE VO CUES
### Cue 1: The Grandmother's Warning (Tumbbad Style)
- **Devanagari:** दादी कहती थी... जब भगवान ने सृष्टि बनाई, तो अपने पहले बेटे को भूख दे दी। इतनी भूख कि वह सारा संसार निगल जाना चाहता था। [PAUSE: 1.2s] उसका नाम कभी पूजा में नहीं लिया जाता... मगर उस पुरानी हवेली के तहखाने में, वह आज भी अंधेरे में सांस ले रहा है, सोने के सिक्कों पर लेटा हुआ... अपनी अगली खुराक के इंतज़ार में।
- **Subtext:** Mythological foundation of primordial greed as a sleeping cosmic parasite.

### Cue 2: The Cellar Descent
- **Devanagari:** सीढ़ियां नीचे उतरती थीं... जहां कभी सूरज की किरण नहीं पहुंची। [PAUSE: 0.8s] हवा में नम आटे और सड़े हुए मांस की ऐसी गंध थी जो सांस नली में चिपक जाती थी। हाथ में लालटेन थी... और सामने वह लाल मखमली कोख, जहां से सदियों पहले का भूखा देवता जागने वाला था।
- **Subtext:** The subterranean descent into maternal/biological terror.

### Cue 3: The Fatal Trap of Greed
- **Devanagari:** संदूक खुला। सोना चमक उठा। [PAUSE: 1.0s] उसने दोनों हाथों से सिक्के भरे... मगर जब मुड़ा, तो दरवाजे का भारी पत्थर अपनी जगह गिर चुका था। बाहर जाने का कोई रास्ता नहीं था। अब उस अंधेरे तहखाने में सिर्फ़ दो चीज़ें बची थीं... एक भूखा देवता, और एक नया कैदी जो खुद उसका खाना बनने वाला था।
- **Subtext:** The inescapable tomb sealed by the weight of human avarice.
