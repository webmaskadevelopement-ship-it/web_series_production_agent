# GENRE PRODUCTION PLAYBOOK: ACTION: GRITTY TACTICAL SURVIVAL & REVENGE (`13_action_gritty_tactical_survival_and_revenge.md`)
*(Bone-Dry Physicality, Breath, Blood, Mud & Primal Survival Drive)*

## 1. GENRE PHILOSOPHY & NARRATIVE CONTRACT
In tactical survival and raw revenge action (The Raid, Extraction, NH10), action is not a superhero myth; it is an agonizing, bone-breaking biological ordeal. Every punch cracks cartilage, every bullet wound burns with infection, boots stick in heavy mud, and the protagonist fights with the desperate, feral instinct of a cornered animal trying to breathe for one more minute.

---

## 2. RECOMMENDED WRITER BRAIN BLEND
- **Saadat Hasan Manto (50%):** Stripped biological reality, cold surgical trauma, naked facts.
- **Tigmanshu Dhulia (35%):** Visceral aggression, rustic weapons, survival instinct.
- **Anurag Kashyap (15%):** Kinetic breath, mud, bleeding adrenaline.

---

## 3. ETYMOLOGICAL & DIALECT REGISTER SPEC
- **Register:** Raw Kinetic Hindi `60%` + Tactical Military/Street Slang `30%` + Deshaj `10%`.
- **Vocabulary:** सांस, फेफड़े, कीचड़, खून, हड्डी, चाकू, पसीना, नब्ज़, घाव, टांके, गोली, फावड़ा.
- **Banned:** Flowery poetry, philosophical monologues during combat, fake movie bravado.

---

## 4. SYNTACTIC PACING & WPM ENVELOPE
- **Pacing:** 90–112 WPM. Breath-heavy, fractured, kinetic.
- **Average Sentence Length:** 4–7 words.
- **Pause Structure:** Sudden ragged inhale/exhale tags (`[HEAVY_EXHALE: 0.4s]`).

---

## 5. ACOUSTIC & VOCAL TIMBRE DIRECTIVE
- **Vocal Timbre:** Strained, hoarse, exhausted (F0: 105–125 Hz). Laryngeal compression.
- **Mic Placement:** Dynamic mic at 10 cm, capturing lip moisture and ragged breath transients.
- **DSP Chain:** Empirical Labs Distressor, aggressive transient shaping, zero reverb.

## 7. 5 STRUCTURAL SCENE ARCHETYPES FOR THIS GENRE
1. **The Trap Closes:** The protagonist cornered in an industrial ruin or dense jungle with nowhere to run.
2. **The Improvised Weapon:** Finding a rusted pipe, a broken bottle, or a shovel to defend life.
3. **The Brutal Hand-to-Hand:** A silent, exhausting, ugly grapple in mud where both combatants bleed.
4. **The Field Triage:** Cauterizing a wound with gunpowder or sewing flesh with fishing wire.
5. **The Sole Survivor's Stumble:** Crawling out of the carnage into dawn light, half-dead.

---

## 8. CONTRAPUNTAL FRAME-TO-VOICE RULES
- Never celebrate victory; describe the agonizing exhaustion of simply remaining alive.
- Focus on the physical mechanical resistance of objects (jammed triggers, slippery bloody grips).
- Every verb must be physical, violent, present-tense, and short.

---

## 9. GENRE CLICHÉ TRAPS TO AVOID
- ❌ Clean martial arts heroics where nobody gets tired.
- ❌ Speaking during close combat; real fighters hold breath.
- ❌ Heroic speeches after getting stabbed in the abdomen.

---

## 10. 3 FULL PRODUCTION-GRADE SAMPLE VO CUES
### Cue 1: Cornered in the Alley
- **Devanagari:** रास्ता बंद था। [INHALE_RAGGED: 0.3s] सामने तीन लोग थे, हाथ में लोहे की सरिया। फेफड़ों में हवा खत्म हो चुकी थी। उसने जमीन से आधी टूटी हुई ईंट उठाई... उंगलियों में कांच चुभा, मगर दर्द महसूस नहीं हुआ। अब सिर्फ़ एक ही नियम था—मारो, या यहीं कीचड़ में सड़ जाओ।
- **Subtext:** The instantaneous transition from civilian panic to feral animal survival.

### Cue 2: The Grapple in the Mud
- **Devanagari:** दोनों कीचड़ में गिरे। सांसें टकरा रही थीं। [PAUSE: 0.4s] उसका हाथ मेरी गर्दन पर था... हलक में हवा रुक चुकी थी। मैंने हाथ बढ़ाया... कीचड़ में उंगलियों ने एक नुकीला पत्थर टटोला। एक वार। दूसरा वार। गर्दन की पकड़ ढीली पड़ी... और गर्म खून मेरे चेहरे पर आ गिरा।
- **Subtext:** The unglamorous, visceral, choking reality of close-quarters lethal struggle.

### Cue 3: The Aftermath of Survival
- **Devanagari:** सन्नाटा छा गया। [EXHALE_LONG: 0.5s] तीनों लाशें वहीं पड़ी थीं। उसने घुटनों के बल बैठकर मिट्टी की उल्टी की। हाथ इतने कांप रहे थे कि जेब से रुमाल तक नहीं निकला। वह जीता नहीं था... वह तो बस एक और सांस लेने के लिए इस नरक से बाहर रेंग रहा था।
- **Subtext:** Survival stripped of glory; pure biological exhaustion and nausea.
