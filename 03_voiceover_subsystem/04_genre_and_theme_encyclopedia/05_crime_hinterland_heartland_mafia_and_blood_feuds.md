# GENRE PRODUCTION PLAYBOOK: CRIME: HINTERLAND HEARTLAND MAFIA & BLOOD FEUDS (`05_crime_hinterland_heartland_mafia_and_blood_feuds.md`)
*(Purvanchal/Chambal Coal & Gunpowder Saga, Sand Mafia & Feudal Grudges)*

## 1. GENRE PHILOSOPHY & NARRATIVE CONTRACT
In hinterland heartland crime (Wasseypur, Mirzapur, Paan Singh Tomar), crime is not a sleek modern choice; it is a hereditary curse rooted in land, sand tenders, coal sidings, and caste dominance. The voiceover is a sardonic, tobacco-spitting town gossip sitting on a wooden bench watching families slaughter each other over half an acre of gravel or an election booth.

---

## 2. RECOMMENDED WRITER BRAIN BLEND
- **Piyush Mishra (45%):** Folk-punk rhythm, sardonic balladry, coal-and-gunpowder swagger.
- **Tigmanshu Dhulia (35%):** Feudal machismo, dry threats, institutional cynicism.
- **Shrilal Shukla (20%):** Raag Darbari dark bureaucratic mockery of rural democracy.

---

## 3. ETYMOLOGICAL & DIALECT REGISTER SPEC
- **Register:** Raw Purvanchali/Bhojpuri/Awadhi Deshaj `50%` + Heartland Hindi `35%` + Feudal Urdu `15%`.
- **Vocabulary:** कट्टा, भौकाल, रंगदारी, प्रधानी, ठेका, वर्चस्व, छर्रा, बही, खादी, दरोगा, बीहड़, लाठी.
- **Banned:** Polite urban drawing-room etiquette, generic Hollywood gangster lines.

---

## 4. SYNTACTIC PACING & WPM ENVELOPE
- **Pacing:** 110–128 WPM. Kinetic, syncopated, percussive.
- **Average Sentence Length:** 6–9 words.
- **Pause Structure:** Dramatic 0.8s syncopated cuts right before a gunshot or political betrayal.

---

## 5. ACOUSTIC & VOCAL TIMBRE DIRECTIVE
- **Vocal Timbre:** Abrasive, tobacco-cured, percussive (F0: 110–135 Hz).
- **Mic Placement:** Dynamic or condenser at 15 cm, 15° off-axis to handle plosives.
- **DSP Chain:** Empirical Labs Distressor (fast attack, 4:1 ratio), bite EQ at 3.2 kHz (+3 dB).

## 7. 5 STRUCTURAL SCENE ARCHETYPES FOR THIS GENRE
1. **The Origin Feud:** A petty insult over half an inch of boundary land igniting 30 years of slaughter.
2. **The First Murder:** An awkward youth pulling a country pistol trigger, shaking with sweat.
3. **The Industrial Capture:** Taking over the local sand mining or railway transport racket with political backing.
4. **The Feast Before Slaughter:** A wedding feast where rivals eat together while planning ambushes.
5. **The Inevitable Drainage Ditch Death:** The feared warlord gunned down in flip-flops in a muddy alley.

---

## 8. CONTRAPUNTAL FRAME-TO-VOICE RULES
- Over a gruesome shootout, VO reads the prices of the vegetables the killer's wife bought that morning.
- Over political speechifying about rural development, VO describes the sand dumper crushing a bridge.
- Maintain sardonic, mocking distance; never celebrate the gangsters as noble heroes.

---

## 9. GENRE CLICHÉ TRAPS TO AVOID
- ❌ Glorifying gangsters as stylish superheroes.
- ❌ Generic Mumbai Tapori language used in a Bihar/UP village setting.
- ❌ Preachy moral sermons at the end of the scene.

---

## 10. 3 FULL PRODUCTION-GRADE SAMPLE VO CUES
### Cue 1: The Origin of the Feud
- **Devanagari:** बबूल के उस पेड़ के नीचे... झगड़ा सिर्फ़ तीन बित्ते ज़मीन का था। [PAUSE: 0.6s] मगर खानदान की मूंछ इतनी बड़ी थी कि झुक नहीं सकती थी। उस रोज़ जो पहली लाठी चली... उसने तीस साल का ऐसा खूनी खाता खोल दिया जिसमें आज तक सिर्फ़ लाशों की एंट्री हो रही है।
- **Subtext:** Generational slaughter triggered by petty spatial entitlement.

### Cue 2: The Contract Killing
- **Devanagari:** लड़का उन्नीस साल का था। कट्टा नया था, मगर हाथ कांप रहे थे। [PAUSE: 0.4s] उसने घोड़ा दबाया... और छर्रा सीधे चाचा की कनपटी में जा घुसा। खून नाली में बह गया, और गांव ने समझ लिया कि अब चौपाल पर कौन-सा नया मालिक बैठने वाला है।
- **Subtext:** The bloody transition of patriarchal authority in the hinterland.

### Cue 3: The End of Swagger
- **Devanagari:** पूरा जिला थर्राता था उस नाम से। [PAUSE: 0.8s] मगर जब गोली लगी, तो विधायक जी की जीप के पहिए के नीचे गिरे पड़े थे... एक पैर में टूटी हुई चप्पल थी, और मुंह में सुरती का चूना। सारा भौकाल दो कौड़ी के कीचड़ में मिलकर बह गया।
- **Subtext:** The grotesque, unglamorous reality of hinterland violent death.
