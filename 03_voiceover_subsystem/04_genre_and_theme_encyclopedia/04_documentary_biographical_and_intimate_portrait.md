# GENRE PRODUCTION PLAYBOOK: DOCUMENTARY: BIOGRAPHICAL & INTIMATE PORTRAIT (`04_documentary_biographical_and_intimate_portrait.md`)
*(Psychological Excavation, Archival Diary Intimacy & Humanizing Contradictions)*

## 1. GENRE PHILOSOPHY & NARRATIVE CONTRACT
In biographical portraiture, the voiceover acts as an empathetic excavator of the subject's private doubts behind their public monument. It refuses hagiography (worship) and demonization, exploring the fragile, contradictory, often deeply wounded private self. The cadence is that of reading from unposted letters and intimate diary margins late at night.

---

## 2. RECOMMENDED WRITER BRAIN BLEND
- **Neelesh Misra (45%):** Conversational warmth, domestic pauses, empathetic curiosity.
- **Agyeya (35%):** Psychological interiority, existential second-guessing, intellectual nuance.
- **Gulzar (20%):** Tactile sensory memory, unraveled moments, domestic textures.

---

## 3. ETYMOLOGICAL & DIALECT REGISTER SPEC
- **Register:** Intimate Hindustani `60%` + Literary Khari Boli `30%` + Rekhta Nuance `10%`.
- **Vocabulary:** खामोशी, डायरी, बचपन, संशय, कमरा, चिट्ठी, अकेलेपन, आदत, चश्मा, सफर, चेहरा, विरासत.
- **Banned:** Generic hero-worship, superficial Wikipedia timeline narration.

---

## 4. SYNTACTIC PACING & WPM ENVELOPE
- **Pacing:** 88–98 WPM. Soft, reflective, unhurried cadence.
- **Average Sentence Length:** 8–11 words.
- **Pause Structure:** Intimate 1.0s holds after revealing a vulnerable flaw or private confession.

---

## 5. ACOUSTIC & VOCAL TIMBRE DIRECTIVE
- **Vocal Timbre:** Warm, vulnerable, conversational (F0: 100–114 Hz). Proximity mask.
- **Mic Placement:** Neumann U87 at 10 cm, cardioid, close vocal intimacy.
- **DSP Chain:** Pultec low-end warmth (+2 dB @ 100 Hz), smooth 3:1 optical compression.

## 7. 5 STRUCTURAL SCENE ARCHETYPES FOR THIS GENRE
1. **The Private Domestic Habit:** Introducing the legend through an eccentric, quiet morning routine.
2. **The Formative Wound:** The childhood loss or humiliation that created their unyielding drive.
3. **The Crucible of Doubt:** The period where everyone abandoned them and they contemplated giving up.
4. **The Public Zenith vs Private Void:** World acclaim juxtaposed with broken domestic relationships.
5. **The Final Solitude:** The aging icon in a quiet room, stripped of public adulation.

---

## 8. CONTRAPUNTAL FRAME-TO-VOICE RULES
- Never celebrate an award on screen with celebratory words; speak of the insomnia that followed.
- Contrast the thunderous applause of stadiums with the silence of their hotel room.
- Use the physical artifacts of their trade (a typewriter ribbon, a medical stethoscope) as emotional mirrors.

---

## 9. GENRE CLICHÉ TRAPS TO AVOID
- ❌ Hollow hagiography (*'वे एक मसीहा थे'*).
- ❌ Ignoring their human flaws, addictions, or failed promises.
- ❌ Wikipedia-style date dumping without psychological insight.

## 10. 3 FULL PRODUCTION-GRADE SAMPLE VO CUES
### Cue 1: The Public Icon vs Private Doubt
- **Devanagari:** दुनिया जिसे क्रांति का मसीहा मानती थी... वह रात को तीन बजे उठकर अपनी डायरी में लिखता था—'मुझे डर लगता है कि कहीं लोग मेरा असली चेहरा न देख लें।' [PAUSE: 1.0s] वह चेहरा किसी फौलाद का नहीं था... वह तो एक थके हुए इंसान का था, जो अपनी ही बनाई प्रतिमा का बोझ ढोते-ढोते टूट चुका था।
- **Subtext:** The crushing psychic burden of living as a public symbol.

### Cue 2: The Scent of Old Ink
- **Devanagari:** उस पुरानी मेज पर... आज भी काली रोशनाई का एक गोल धब्बा मौजूद है। [PAUSE: 0.8s] यहीं बैठकर उसने वे कहानियां लिखी थीं जिन्होंने एक पूरे मुल्क को हिलाकर रख दिया। मगर जब वह लिखता था, तो हाथ कांपते थे... क्योंकि हर लफ़्ज़ के पीछे उसके अपने घर की फाकाकशी की चीख छिपी होती थी।
- **Subtext:** Material deprivation fueling creative fire.

### Cue 3: The Final Quiet Breath
- **Devanagari:** अस्पताल के उस जनरल वार्ड में... जब उसने अंतिम सांस ली, तो तकिए के नीचे सिर्फ़ एक मुड़ी हुई चिट्ठी मिली। [PAUSE: 1.2s] कोई वसीयत नहीं, कोई दौलत नहीं... सिर्फ़ एक मां का नाम, और एक अधूरा शेर। जिंदगी अपनी पूरी रफ्तार के बाद वहीं लौट आई थी, जहां से शुरू हुई थी।
- **Subtext:** Ultimate return to stripped biological vulnerability.
