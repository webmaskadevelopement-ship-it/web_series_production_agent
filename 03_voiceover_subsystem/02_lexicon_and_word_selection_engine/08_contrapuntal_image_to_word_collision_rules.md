# CONTRAPUNTAL IMAGE-TO-WORD COLLISION RULES (`08_contrapuntal_image_to_word_collision_rules.md`)

## 1. THE CARDINAL RULE: NEVER DESCRIBE THE FRAME
If the camera is showing a man crying while holding a broken photograph, the voiceover must NEVER say *"वह रो रहा था और तस्वीर को देख रहा था"* (He was crying and looking at the picture). This is redundant, insulting to the viewer's intelligence, and destroys cinematic immersion.

The Voiceover and the Visual Frame must exist in a **Contrapuntal (Counterpoint) Relationship**:
- When the visual is **Violent**, the voiceover must be **Mundane, Whispered, or Philosophical**.
- When the visual is **Festive / Joyous**, the voiceover must register the **Approaching Shadow of Catastrophe**.
- When the visual is **Silent & Static**, the voiceover must unleash the **Internal Storm of the Mind**.
