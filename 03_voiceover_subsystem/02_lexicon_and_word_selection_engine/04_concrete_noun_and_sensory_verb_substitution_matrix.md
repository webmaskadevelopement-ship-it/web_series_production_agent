# CONCRETE NOUN & SENSORY VERB SUBSTITUTION MATRIX (`04_concrete_noun_and_sensory_verb_substitution_matrix.md`)

## 1. THE ERADICATION OF ABSTRACT NOUNS
Abstract nouns (*गरीबी, उदासी, डर, प्यार, गुस्सा*) are cognitive summaries, not sensory cinema. An audience cannot see or hear "poverty"; they can only see a rusted tin roof held down by four bricks, or hear the dry scrape of an empty spoon on a blackened iron pot.

This matrix contains **50+ Concrete Noun & Sensory Verb Substitution Formulas** designed to mechanically eliminate abstract vocabulary from Voiceover scripts.
