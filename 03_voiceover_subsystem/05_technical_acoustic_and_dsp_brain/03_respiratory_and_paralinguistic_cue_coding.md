# RESPIRATORY & PARALINGUISTIC CUE CODING (`03_respiratory_and_paralinguistic_cue_coding.md`)

## 1. THE NON-VERBAL MATRIX OF AUTHENTICITY
Human speech is biologically inseparable from respiration and autonomic nervous system reactions. A synthesized or directed voiceover that speaks without breathing sounds uncanny, synthetic, and emotionally dead.

The Voiceover Subsystem defines a **Formal Paralinguistic Notation Standard** embedded directly into the master script cues.

---

## 2. THE SEVEN PARALINGUISTIC OPERATORS

1. **`[INHALE_NASAL: Xs]` (बंद मुंह की सांस):**
   - Inhalation through the nose with closed lips. Communicates interiority, private thought, or holding back tears. Duration: 0.3s to 0.6s.
2. **`[INHALE_ORAL_SHALLOW: Xs]` (मुख-श्वास / घबराहट):**
   - Rapid mouth intake through slightly parted lips. Communicates shock, cold temperature, or sudden fear. Duration: 0.2s to 0.4s.
3. **`[EXHALE_TRAIL: Xs]` (निश्वास / थकान):**
   - Audible unvoiced air escaping at the end of a clause. Fundamental pitch drops into a sigh. Communicates resignation, aging, or exhaustion.
4. **`[GLOTTAL_HOLD: Xs]` (कंठ-अवरोध / हिचकिचाहट):**
   - Sudden closure of vocal folds holding back air pressure. Communicates guilt, terror, or the struggle to confess a shameful truth.
5. **`[VOCAL_FRY]` (कंठ की खुरदुराहट / क्रीक):**
   - Sub-harmonic creaky voice (below 70 Hz) where vocal folds vibrate loosely. Conveys old age, early morning waking, or terminal physical exhaustion.
6. **`[LIP_PART_CLICK]` (होंठों की शुष्क चटक):**
   - Subtle acoustic transient (`-36 dBFS` to `-30 dBFS`) caused by saliva parting when dry lips open before speaking. Conveys thirst, dehydration, or intense anxiety.
7. **`[THROAT_CLEAR_SUPPRESSED]` (दबा हुआ खंखार):**
   - Low guttural friction in the larynx. Conveys bureaucratic discomfort, moral cowardice, or status assertion.

## 3. FULL PRODUCTION CODED SCRIPT DEMONSTRATION
```text
[LIP_PART_CLICK] कुछ घर ईंट और पत्थर से नहीं... 
[INHALE_NASAL: 0.4s] [THEHRAAV_HOLD: 0.6s] 
अधूरे वादों की सीली नमी से टिके रहते हैं। 
[EXHALE_TRAIL: 0.5s] [VOCAL_FRY]
```
