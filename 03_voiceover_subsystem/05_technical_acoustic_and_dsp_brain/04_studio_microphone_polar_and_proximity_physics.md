# STUDIO MICROPHONE POLAR & PROXIMITY PHYSICS (`04_studio_microphone_polar_and_proximity_physics.md`)

## 1. THE ACOUSTIC TRANSDUCTION ENVIRONMENT
The spatial perspective of the voiceover must match the camera's psychological distance. A grand, epic voiceover recorded 5cm from the microphone capsule creates grotesque claustrophobia; an intimate deathbed whisper recorded 30cm away in a reverberant room sounds like a public announcement.

---

## 2. THE THREE CANONICAL STUDIO MICROPHONES

### 1. NEUMANN U87 Ai (LARGE DIAPHRAGM CONDENSER)
- **Acoustic Character:** Warm, velvety low-mids with a silky presence rise (+2 dB @ 10 kHz).
- **Polar Pattern:** Cardioid.
- **Recommended Distance:** 12cm to 15cm from pop filter.
- **Best For:** Retrospective Confessional, Family Melancholy, Gulzar/Premchand Emulations.
- **Proximity Note:** Engages chest resonance (100–180 Hz) without muddy boom when angled 15° off-axis.

### 2. SCHOEPS MK41 (SUPERCARDIOID SMALL DIAPHRAGM CONDENSER)
- **Acoustic Character:** Incorruptible linear frequency response; zero off-axis coloration.
- **Polar Pattern:** Supercardioid.
- **Recommended Distance:** 20cm to 25cm.
- **Best For:** Satyajit Ray Intellectual Detachment, Forensic Crime Procedural, Investigative Documentary.

### 3. SENNHEISER MKH 416 (SHORT RIFLE SHOTGUN)
- **Acoustic Character:** High-transient bite, laser-focused rejection of ambient room reflections, boosted 3 kHz–5 kHz presence.
- **Polar Pattern:** Supercardioid / Lobar.
- **Recommended Distance:** 18cm to 22cm.
- **Best For:** Hinterland Crime, Anurag Kashyap / Piyush Mishra grit, Action Mass Elevation.
