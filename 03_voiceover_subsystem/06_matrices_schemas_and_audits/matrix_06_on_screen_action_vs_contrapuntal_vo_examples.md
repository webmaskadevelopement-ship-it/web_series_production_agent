## 3. THREE-QUESTION AUDIT GATE FOR EVERY CUE
1. If the audio is muted, does the viewer already know what the bad voiceover is saying? *(If YES -> Delete/Rewrite)*.
2. Does the voiceover provide an invisible historical, financial, or psychological dimension that the camera CANNOT photograph? *(Must be YES)*.
3. Does the emotion emerge from the **friction** between the image and the voice? *(Must be YES)*.
