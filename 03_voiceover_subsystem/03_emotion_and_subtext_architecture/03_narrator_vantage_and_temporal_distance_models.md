# NARRATOR VANTAGE & TEMPORAL DISTANCE MODELS (`03_narrator_vantage_and_temporal_distance_models.md`)

## 1. THE GEOMETRY OF NARRATIVE DISTANCE
The voiceover is never a neutral, disembodied loudspeaker; it is a **specific consciousness speaking from a precise coordinate in space, time, and moral culpability.** If the temporal distance is undefined, the voiceover will drift into generic television commentary.

The Voiceover Subsystem enforces **Six Canonical Vantage Models**:
1. **The Retrospective Confessional (20-Years-Later Regret)**
2. **The Dying Confession (In-Articulo-Mortis)**
3. **The Omniscient City / Geological Witness (Choric Consciousness)**
4. **The Cynical Accomplice (The Streetwise Insider)**
5. **The Uncomprehending Child's Eye (Innocence as Scalpel)**
6. **The Forensic Autopsy Observer (The Ledger of Facts)**

---

## 2. DEEP SPECIFICATION OF THE 6 VANTAGE MODELS

### VANTAGE 01: THE RETROSPECTIVE CONFESSIONAL (20-YEARS-LATER REGRET)
- **Temporal Distance:** 15 to 30 years post-tragedy. The narrator is an older survivor who knows how every choice unraveled.
- **Psychological Posture:** Forgiving of others, merciless toward their own younger self. Zero vanity remaining.
- **Pronoun Architecture:** Distances the narrator from their past self by using third-person or alienated first-person (*"उस चौंतीस साल के लड़के को लगता था कि..."*).
- **Acoustic Signature:** 82–90 WPM | Weathered baritone (95–115 Hz) | Extended pauses on admissions of cowardice.
- **Worked Cue:**
  - *Devanagari:* "उस शाम जब मैं उस स्टेशन से निकला था, तो मुझे लगा कि मैं आज़ाद हो गया। तीस साल लगे यह समझने में... कि आदमी जिस घर को छोड़कर भागता है, ताउम्र उसी की चौखट पर ठोकरें खाता रहता है।"
  - *Subtext:* The illusion of youth unmasked by decades of wandering.

### VANTAGE 02: THE DYING CONFESSION (IN-ARTICULO-MORTIS)
- **Temporal Distance:** Minutes or seconds before death. The narrator is bleeding out in an alley, lying on a hospital bed, or facing the gallows.
- **Psychological Posture:** Complete eradication of social masks. Biological urgency colliding with terminal fatigue.
- **Syntactic Architecture:** Fragmented clauses, gasps, unvoiced exhalations, sentences left incomplete as breath fails.
- **Acoustic Signature:** 65–75 WPM | Whispered vocal fry | Audible dry lip separations (`-32 dBFS`).
- **Worked Cue:**
  - *Devanagari:* "सांस... भारी हो रही है। अब कोई फ़र्क़ नहीं पड़ता कि तिजोरी में कितना सोना बचा था... [INHALE: 0.8s] बस उस कुएं का पानी... एक बार पीने को मिल जाता।"
  - *Subtext:* Material empires vanishing before biological dehydration.

### VANTAGE 03: THE OMNISCIENT CITY / GEOLOGICAL WITNESS (CHORIC CONSCIOUSNESS)
- **Temporal Distance:** Millennia or centuries. The narrator is the city itself (Kashi, Mumbai, Delhi) or the river (Ganga, Yamuna).
- **Psychological Posture:** Ancient, biocentric, unmoved by human hysterics. Treats kings, gangsters, and beggars as passing microbes.
- **Lexicon:** High Tatsama or classical Ganga-Jamuni Urdu. Architectural and geological nouns (चट्टान, मुहाने, गाद, राख, सदियां).
- **Acoustic Signature:** 78–86 WPM | Deep spatial reverb (Altiverb 8 Cathedral / Stone Ghat impulse) | Resonant low-end warmth.
- **Worked Cue:**
  - *Devanagari:* "मैंने इन घाटों पर हरिश्चंद्र को भी रोते देखा है, और औरंगज़ेब के घोड़ों की टापें भी सुनी हैं। यह जो नया सुल्तान आया है... इसे लगता है कि पत्थर इसके हुक्म से मुड़ते हैं। कल इसकी भी राख इसी पानी में बह जाएगी।"
  - *Subtext:* The sublime indifference of geography to political tyranny.

### VANTAGE 04: THE CYNICAL ACCOMPLICE (THE STREETWISE INSIDER)
- **Temporal Distance:** Real-time to immediate past (hours/days). Sitting across the table at an illegal country-liquor shack.
- **Psychological Posture:** Amoral, survivalist, darkly humorous. Does not judge the crime because they helped dig the trench.
- **Lexicon:** Pure Deshaj / Tapori / Heartland dialect. High slang density (भौकाल, चू**या, कांड, खोपचा).
- **Acoustic Signature:** 115–130 WPM | Staccato, aggressive pacing | Close proximity (8cm).
- **Worked Cue:**
  - *Devanagari:* "अरे भैया, क़ानून-फ़ानून तो किताबों में अच्छा लगता है। यहां जब रात को कट्टा बजता है न, तो बड़े-बड़े दरोगा अपनी वर्दी संभालते-संभालते थाने का पिछला फाटक ढूंढते हैं। हम तो बस ड्राइवर थे... हमने गाड़ी मोड़ी और निकल लिए।"
  - *Subtext:* The pragmatism of the accomplice who survives by staying unimportant.

### VANTAGE 05: THE UNCOMPREHENDING CHILD'S EYE (INNOCENCE AS SCALPEL)
- **Temporal Distance:** Real-time present tense. A 7-to-10-year-old observing adult brutality without adult concepts.
- **Psychological Posture:** Innocent, literal, hyper-observant of colors, smells, and sounds. Unaware of tragedy.
- **Syntactic Architecture:** Simple compound sentences linked by *"और फिर"*. No moral adjectives.
- **Acoustic Signature:** 90–100 WPM | Light vocal weight, clear diction, absence of cynical subtext in the delivery.
- **Worked Cue:**
  - *Devanagari:* "अम्मा ने अपनी लाल चूड़ियां ज़मीन पर पटक दीं। बहुत सुंदर आवाज़ आई थी, जैसे दिवाली का पटाखा। फिर सब लोग रोने लगे... और बाबूजी सफेद चादर ओढ़कर सो गए। मैंने सोचा बाबूजी को ठंड लग रही है।"
  - *Subtext:* The shattering irony of funeral rituals observed by a child who thinks it is a game.

### VANTAGE 06: THE FORENSIC AUTOPSY OBSERVER (THE LEDGER OF FACTS)
- **Temporal Distance:** Post-investigative. An archivist, cold-case detective, or forensic pathologist reading evidence.
- **Psychological Posture:** Clinical, unblinking, zero moralizing. Facts, timestamps, and measurements are weaponized.
- **Lexicon:** Institutional Hindustani, legal and medical terminology.
- **Acoustic Signature:** 100–110 WPM | Flat fundamental pitch (minimal melodic variance) | Dead acoustic dry booth.
- **Worked Cue:**
  - *Devanagari:* "तारीख: 14 अगस्त। समय: रात के 11 बजकर 42 मिनट। उस कमरे का पंखा नंबर तीन पर चल रहा था। मेज़ पर दो कप चाय थी, दोनों आधी पी गई थीं। तीसरी कुर्सी पर खून के छींटे थे... जो यह साबित करने के लिए काफ़ी थे कि बातचीत बेनतीजा रही थी।"
  - *Subtext:* The unsparing indictment of crime through physical evidence.
