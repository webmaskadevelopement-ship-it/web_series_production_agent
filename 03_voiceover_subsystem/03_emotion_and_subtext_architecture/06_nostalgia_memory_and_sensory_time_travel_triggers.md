# NOSTALGIA, MEMORY & SENSORY TIME-TRAVEL TRIGGERS (`06_nostalgia_memory_and_sensory_time_travel_triggers.md`)

## 1. THE PROUSTIAN SENSORY ENGINE IN THE INDIAN CONTEXT
Marcel Proust's *Madeleine* in Indian cinema is never an aristocratic tea biscuit; it is the smell of **first rain on scorched red soil (*सोंधी मिट्टी / Petrichor*)**, the whistling static of an old **Murphy radio** tuning into Radio Ceylon, or the sharp scent of **mustard oil sizzling with fenugreek seeds** in a courtyard at 5 AM.

Memory in voiceover should never be triggered by an abstract thought ("I remembered my childhood"). It MUST be detonated by a **Sensory Intrusion** that breaches the character's defenses.

---

## 2. THE 5 SENSORY MEMORY TRIGGER DOMAINS

### DOMAIN 1: OLFACTORY & ATMOSPHERIC TRIGGERS (गंध और आबोहवा)
1. **भीगे खपरैल की गंध (Wet Terracotta Roof Tiles):** Triggers memories of monsoon childhoods, family huddled on verandahs, and leaking roofs.
2. **कपूर और फिनाइल का सम्मिश्रण (Camphor & Phenyl):** Triggers memories of government hospitals, dying grandparents, and sterile morgues.
3. **ताज़ा छिला कच्चा आम और नमक (Raw Mango & Salt):** Triggers childhood truancy, stolen afternoons in orchards, and maternal reprimand.
4. **धूप में सूखती कड़वी मिर्च (Sun-Drying Red Chillies):** Triggers courtyard family gatherings, sharp domestic friction, and women's whispered secrets.

### DOMAIN 2: ACOUSTIC MEMORY GHOSTS (ध्वनि के प्रेत)
1. **दोपहर की दूर आती कुल्फ़ी वाले की घंटी (Afternoon Kulfi Bell):** The immense, empty loneliness of 2 PM in a North Indian small town.
2. **साइकिल के पायदान की चूं-चूं (Rhythmical Bicycle Pedal Creak):** Father returning late from the sugar mill, tired and defeated.
3. **छत पर कबूतरों के फड़फड़ाने की आवाज़ (Pigeon Wings on Parapet):** Old ancestral havelis slipping into decrepitude.
4. **दूर से गुजरती मालगाड़ी का हॉर्न (Distant Freight Train Horn at 3 AM):** The ache of confinement in a landlocked provincial town.

### DOMAIN 3: TACTILE & TEXTILE RESIDUALS (स्पर्श की स्मृतियां)
1. **खादी के कुर्ते की खुरदुराहट (Starch-Stiff Khadi Texture):** Ideological integrity, political meetings, and austere grandfathers.
2. **धूप में तपता लोहे का हैंडपंप (Sun-Baked Iron Handpump Handle):** Scarcity of water, blistering summer afternoons, and physical resilience.
3. **अलमारी के दराज की अटकी हुई लकड़ी (Swollen Wooden Drawer in Monsoon):** Hidden secrets, old love letters, and family documents.

## 3. MASTER VO PRODUCTION CUE: THE MEMORY CASCADE
- **Visual:** Raghav sits in a high-rise glass boardroom in Gurgaon; a worker outside cleans the glass with a damp squeegee.
- **Audio VO:**
  - *Devanagari:* "शीशे पर रबर के उस वाइपर की घिसट ने... अचानक मुझे पच्चीस साल पीछे फेंक दिया। ठीक ऐसी ही आवाज़ बाबूजी के चश्मे के शीशे पोंछने की थी... जब वे हर इतवार की सुबह अपने फटे हुए कुर्ते की आस्तीन से धूल साफ़ किया करते थे।"
  - *Subtext:* Corporate triumph instantly punctured by the tactile poverty of his origins.
