# PRESET 02 — GROUNDED POLICE PROCEDURAL

## Use This Preset

Use when the documentary must be easy to follow, credible, and process-led: investigations, surveillance, arrests, evidence, command decisions, and accountability.

## Voice Direction

- **Persona:** Calm investigator or documentary narrator who respects facts more than spectacle.
- **Age / energy:** 35–55; steady, attentive, precise.
- **Timbre:** Clean mid-low voice with firm consonants and low emotional wobble.
- **Emotional movement:** Neutral setup → focused concern → controlled urgency → factual consequence → measured conclusion.
- **Dialect:** Clear contemporary Hindi; retain only familiar operational English terms such as Police, Crime Branch, surveillance, driver, or report.

## Writing Recipe

1. State the public problem in one clear sentence.
2. Give the essential who, where, when, and why.
3. Explain the investigation as a chain of observable steps.
4. Use short paragraphs, each with one action or decision.
5. Describe violence without graphic flourish; state the consequence and record-keeping.
6. Show the institution correcting, learning, or accepting responsibility.
7. End with a plain civic takeaway that the audience can repeat.

## Prosody and Timing

- **Target:** 88–96 WPM; 555–625 words for 6.5 minutes only when pauses are light. For a slower 6.5-minute cut, use 560–575 words at 88–89 WPM.
- **Pitch:** Moderate range; small rise on new information, controlled drop at verified conclusions.
- **Pauses:** 3–6 pauses; short pause 0.25–0.45s; long pause rarely above 1.2s.
- **Sentence shape:** Mostly 8–16 words, with occasional 3–5 word verdicts.
- **Stress:** Stress names, dates, ranks, evidence, and decisions rather than adjectives.

## Cue Palette

`[serious]` ` [short pause]` ` [measured]` ` [exhales]` ` [radio texture]` ` [document turn]`

## Anti-Patterns

Do not overload the listener with every procedural detail. Do not use suspense language when a plain fact is stronger. Do not moralize before showing the evidence.

## Prompt Block

```text
Clear Hindi police-procedural documentary narration, evidence-led, calm investigator persona, contemporary Hindi with restrained operational English, concise paragraphs, factual violence, accountable institutions, firm consonants, moderate pitch movement, light pauses, 88–96 WPM.
```
