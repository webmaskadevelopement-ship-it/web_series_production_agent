# PRESET 01 — CIVIC NOIR FIELD WITNESS

## Use This Preset

Use for police operations, manhunts, covert surveillance, dangerous encounters, and episodes where the narrator’s memory carries moral authority.

## Voice Direction

- **Persona:** Experienced field officer remembering the night from close range.
- **Age / energy:** 42–58; physically calm, mentally alert.
- **Timbre:** Dry, low-mid baritone or contralto; restrained grain; no announcer smile.
- **Emotional movement:** Thoughtful → serious → watchful → compressed urgency → quiet moral resolve.
- **Dialect:** Standard Hindi with light Hindustani/Lucknow or North Indian flavor; use English police terms only when operationally natural.

## Writing Recipe

1. Open with a moral contradiction or aphorism.
2. Anchor the listener with place, month/season, year, and atmosphere.
3. Introduce the antagonist through facts, reputation, and one concrete image.
4. Shift into first-person operational memory.
5. Delay the reveal through distance, hands, eyes, doors, tables, or a hidden object.
6. Compress the action into short sentences and one or two diegetic cues.
7. Close by connecting the act to duty, public safety, or the meaning of uniform.

## Prosody and Timing

- **Target:** 82–88 WPM; 540–575 words for 6.5 minutes depending on pause density.
- **Pitch:** Narrow band, approximately 98–116 Hz for a low male voice; 165–195 Hz equivalent for a low female voice.
- **Pauses:** 5–9 intentional pauses; short pause 0.35–0.6s; long pause 1.0–1.8s.
- **Sentence shape:** Use 3–10 word action sentences, followed by one longer sensory or moral sentence.
- **Stress:** Stress concrete nouns and distances: `दस मीटर`, `बंद दरवाज़ा`, `दायाँ हाथ`, `आखिरी गोली`.

## Cue Palette

`[thoughtful]` ` [serious]` ` [whispers]` ` [short pause]` ` [long pause]` ` [exhales sharply]` ` [chuckles]` ` [gunshot]`

## Anti-Patterns

Do not make every line heroic. Do not explain the same danger twice. Do not turn the narrator into an omniscient news anchor. Let the pause carry the fear.

## Prompt Block

