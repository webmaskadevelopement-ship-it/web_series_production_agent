# REFERENCE STYLE ANALYSIS — SUPPLIED VOICEOVER SAMPLE

## Structural Fingerprint

The sample is not a neutral news read. It is a **first-person police-memory documentary** built around a single operation. Its architecture is:

1. A philosophical opening about light, darkness, duty, or danger.
2. A location and date anchor.
3. A compact antagonist profile with reward, reputation, and past violence.
4. A widening institutional frame: department, surveillance, operation.
5. A tactical set piece narrated from inside the officer’s perception.
6. A decisive action beat with diegetic sound.
7. A final reflection that turns a change of clothes into an unchanged moral duty.

## Measured Surface Features

- 3,712 characters in the supplied sample including cue markup.
- 17 sentence-ending units.
- 16 ellipsis groups.
- 8 explicit `[short pause]` or `[long pause]` markers.
- 24 total bracketed performance or sound cues.
- 103 Latin-script tokens inside primarily Hindi narration.

These measurements indicate a deliberately **hybrid Hindi-English operational register** and a performance designed for editing, not just reading aloud.

## Language and Code-Switching

Hindi carries the emotional and moral meaning. English is reserved for recognizable police, restaurant, surveillance, and spatial vocabulary. This makes the narrator sound like an officer remembering real work rather than a literary narrator translating a report. Preserve code-switching only where it improves authenticity or listener comprehension.

## Sentence and Paragraph Behavior

The sample alternates between three sentence modes:

- **Atmospheric mode:** longer clauses, ellipses, sensory temperature, silence, and metaphor.
- **Dossier mode:** names, dates, ranks, rewards, weapons, and verified facts in direct sentences.
- **Tactical mode:** short verbs, distance markers, body-part observations, and rapid cause-and-effect.

The most important cadence rule is contrast: do not sustain one rhythm for the entire episode. Slow down before the reveal, accelerate during the action, then widen into a quiet moral sentence.

## Performance Behavior

The bracket cues create a playable vocal score:

- `[thoughtful]` opens or closes an idea.
- `[serious]` marks verified danger or institutional consequence.
- `[annoyed]` adds controlled judgment without shouting.
- `[excited]` is used for operational urgency, not celebration.
- `[whispers]` narrows the acoustic space around a tactical observation.
- `[exhales sharply]`, `[inhales deeply]`, and `[clears throat]` make the narrator embodied.
- `[gunshot]` is a diegetic editorial event, not a vocal effect.

For a 6.5-minute adaptation, use approximately 5–9 meaningful cues and avoid cueing every sentence.

## What to Preserve in New Episodes

Preserve the witness position, selective English terms, factual specificity, delayed reveal, tactical distance, short action sentences, and a final civic or ethical consequence. Avoid generic motivational language, constant heroic elevation, gratuitous violence, and unexplained technical jargon.

## Failure Findings Added After Comparative Review

The comparative review of the first three generated drafts identified four pipeline failures that are now hard gates:

1. **Duplicate-draft failure:** Simple, polished, and preset-tagged files reused identical prose; a title or tag change is not a creative rewrite.
2. **Helicopter-summary failure:** Thirteen plot beats were compressed into a detached third-person summary, starving the main encounter of time, geometry, and sensory detail.
3. **Telegraphic-staccato failure:** Chained noun fragments and one-line sentences forced repeated TTS pitch resets and sounded like a grocery list.
4. **Mechanical-tag failure:** Mood tags were placed at paragraph index zero, while the reference uses physiological cues inside a thought or action pivot.

The correction is to use a beat-sheet word budget before prose, lock the POV, require source-supported sensory and spatial anchors, retain Roman English for operational Hinglish nouns, use ellipses and em dashes as breath architecture, inject cues mid-paragraph, and run a sibling-similarity and runtime gate before PDF compilation.
