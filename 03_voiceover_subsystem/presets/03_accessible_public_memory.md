# PRESET 03 — ACCESSIBLE PUBLIC MEMORY

## Use This Preset

Use for broad-audience documentaries, public-history episodes, social platforms, and stories where the narration should feel intelligent but immediately understandable.

## Voice Direction

- **Persona:** A trustworthy older sibling, local reporter, or experienced officer explaining what happened to a mixed audience.
- **Age / energy:** 32–52; warm, observant, never patronizing.
- **Timbre:** Natural conversational voice with a soft low register; avoid theatrical bass.
- **Emotional movement:** Curious → concerned → tense → relieved but not celebratory → humane reflection.
- **Dialect:** Simple Khari Boli Hindi with familiar Hindustani words; explain or avoid specialist terms.

## Writing Recipe

1. Begin with a simple image or question the audience understands immediately.
2. Introduce the place and problem without decorative vocabulary.
3. Use one idea per sentence and one event per paragraph.
4. Replace institutional jargon with everyday Hindi unless the exact term matters.
5. Keep names and key facts stable; repeat only the information the audience needs to remember.
6. Let one human detail soften the procedural material.
7. End with a clear, earned public meaning in plain language.

## Prosody and Timing

- **Target:** 86–92 WPM; 555–600 words for 6.5 minutes with natural pauses.
- **Pitch:** Conversational mid-low register; gentle rise for questions and small fall for facts.
- **Pauses:** 4–7 pauses; short pause 0.3–0.5s; long pause 0.8–1.3s.
- **Sentence shape:** 6–14 words; avoid more than two clauses in one sentence.
- **Stress:** Stress the human subject and the consequence: `लोग`, `परिवार`, `सड़क`, `भरोसा`, `जिम्मेदारी`.

## Cue Palette

`[thoughtful]` ` [serious]` ` [small pause]` ` [breath]` ` [quietly]`

## Anti-Patterns

Do not flatten the story into a school lesson. Do not use baby language. Do not add jokes to violence. Do not replace a concrete image with a motivational slogan.

## Prompt Block

```text
Audience-friendly Hindi documentary narration, warm trustworthy storyteller, simple Khari Boli, clear concrete sentences, minimal jargon, human detail, restrained suspense, natural conversational pauses, humane civic ending, 86–92 WPM.
```
