# NARRATOR TEMPORAL STANCE & UNRELIABILITY (`creative_brain/01_narrator_temporal_stance_and_unreliability.md`)

## 1. THE RETROSPECTIVE CONFESSIONAL STANCE
The narrator in this system speaks from a specific point in spacetime:
- **Temporal Anchor:** E.g., Raghav Sharma speaking 20 years after the events, seated in an empty room long after the ancestral haveli has been demolished.
- **Vocal Age & Physiology:** 54 years old; reduced lung vital capacity; subtle laryngeal tension when naming his mother; slight vocal fry on phrase conclusions.
- **Psychological Posture:** Stoic regret masking smoldering guilt; speaking not to explain the plot, but as an act of private confession to an absent listener.
