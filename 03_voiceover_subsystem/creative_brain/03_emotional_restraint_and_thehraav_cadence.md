## 1. THE ARCHITECTURE OF VOCAL UNDERSTATEMENT
High emotional resonance in voiceover is achieved through **Restraint**, not theatrical weeping:
- The narrator withholds vocal pitch spikes, keeping the fundamental frequency ($F_0$) confined to a narrow baritone bandwidth ($98\text{ Hz} – 118\text{ Hz}$).
- Syllable duration stretches on emotionally loaded words (e.g., 'अधूरे' held for $380\text{ ms}$ with breathy aspiration).
- Significant phrases conclude in an unvoiced glottal vocal fry, allowing the ambient Foley and room tone to rush in.
