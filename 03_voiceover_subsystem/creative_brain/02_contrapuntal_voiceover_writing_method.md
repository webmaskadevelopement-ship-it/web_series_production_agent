# CONTRAPUNTAL VOICEOVER WRITING METHOD (`creative_brain/02_contrapuntal_voiceover_writing_method.md`)

## 1. THE CONTRAPUNTAL PRINCIPLE
Amateur voiceover simply narrates what the camera is already showing (e.g., character walks in rain; VO says "It was raining"). 

In professional cinema, voiceover must be **Contrapuntal**:
- The visual shows one reality (Raghav arriving with modern legal papers, dressed in tailored trousers).
- The voiceover delivers an opposing poetic realization: *"कुछ घर ईंट और पत्थर से नहीं... अधूरे वादों की नमी से बने होते हैं।"* (Some homes aren't built of brick and stone... they are built from the dampness of unkept promises).
- The friction between the image (assertive lawyer) and the voice (broken son) generates dramatic irony.
