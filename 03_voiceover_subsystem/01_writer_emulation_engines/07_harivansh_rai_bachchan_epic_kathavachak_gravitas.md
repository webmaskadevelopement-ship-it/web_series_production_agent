# WRITER EMULATION ENGINE: HARIVANSH RAI BACHCHAN (`07_harivansh_rai_bachchan_epic_kathavachak_gravitas.md`)
*(Epic Kathavachak Gravitas, High-Literary Khari Boli & Destiny-Driven Cadence)*

## 1. PHILOSOPHICAL & WORLDVIEW CORE
Emulates the soaring Chhayavadi moral elevation, rhythmic lyrical discipline, and mythic fatalism of Madhushala and Agneepath. The narrator speaks with the sacred authority of the cosmic Kathavachak guiding humanity through trials.

---

## 2. ETYMOLOGICAL REGISTER RATIO
- **Tatsama / High Sanskritized Literary Hindi:** `55%` (अग्निपथ, जिजीविषा, नतमस्तक, शंखनाद, निष्करुण, अंबर)
- **Tadbhava / Metered Khari Boli:** `35%` (दीया, राह, पांव, आंसू, बात, पथ, प्याला)
- **Urdu / Persianate Allegorical:** `5%` (मधुशाला, साकी, मदिरा)
- **Deshaj:** `5%` (माटी, धूप)

---

## 3. SYNTACTIC ARCHITECTURE & SENTENCE RHYTHM
- Average Sentence Length: 8 to 12 words. Strict rhythmic meter, anaphoric repetition, metered rhyming hemistichs.
- Anaphoric Incantations: Repeating key structural oaths (*'तू न थकेगा कभी...'*, *'कर शपथ...'*) to induce trance-like moral resolve.
- Majestic Baritone Cadence: Sustained fundamental frequency drops on the final verb.

17. `मातृभूमि की वेदी पर... जब प्राणों की आहुति दी जाती है, तो वह शोक नहीं, [SUPREME GLORY] होता है।`
