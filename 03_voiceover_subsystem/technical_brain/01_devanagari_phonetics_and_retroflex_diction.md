# DEVANAGARI PHONETICS & RETROFLEX DICTION (`technical_brain/01_devanagari_phonetics_and_retroflex_diction.md`)

## 1. PHONETIC PRECISION ENGINE
The phonetic breakdown in Sub-System 03 maps Devanagari consonants to International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA) standards:
- **Dental Stops:** `t̪` (त), `t̪ʰ` (थ), `d̪` (द), `d̪ʱ` (ध).
- **Retroflex Stops:** `ʈ` (ट), `ʈʰ` (ठ), `ɖ` (ड), `ɖʱ` (ढ), `ɽ` (ड़).
- **Aspirates (Mahaprana):** High airflow volume; requires off-axis microphone rigging to prevent acoustic diaphragm clipping.
- **Nasalization (Anunasika):** `ɦɛ̃ː` (हैं), `ʋɑːd̪oːŋ` (वादों); carries 30% higher breath-to-tone ratio for intimate delivery.
