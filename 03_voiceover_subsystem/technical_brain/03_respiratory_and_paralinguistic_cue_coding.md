# RESPIRATORY & PARALINGUISTIC CUE CODING (`technical_brain/03_respiratory_and_paralinguistic_cue_coding.md`)

## 1. PARALINGUISTIC NOTATION SYSTEM
Vocal emotion lives in the body's involuntary respiratory mechanics. Every voiceover cue in Sub-System 03 incorporates standardized paralinguistic tags:

- `[INHALE_NOSE_0.4s]`: Quiet closed-mouth nasal intake, conveying introspective privacy.
- `[INHALE_MOUTH_SHARP]`: Audible gasping intake, indicating sudden realization or shock.
- `[HOLD_0.5s]`: Glottal suspension holding air prior to painful delivery.
- `[EXHALE_TRAIL_0.6s]`: Slow release of unvoiced air carrying vocal fry.
- `[LIP_PART_DRY]`: Pre-speech acoustic transient ($0.2\text{s}$ before speech) where dry lips separate.
- `[SWALLOW_MICROPULSE]`: Laryngeal swallowing reflex registering suppressed tears.
