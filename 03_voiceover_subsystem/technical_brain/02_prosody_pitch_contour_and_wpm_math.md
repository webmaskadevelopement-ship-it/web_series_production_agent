# PROSODY, PITCH CONTOUR & WPM MATH (`technical_brain/02_prosody_pitch_contour_and_wpm_math.md`)

## 1. ACOUSTIC DELIVERY METRICS
Every voiceover cue card records exact temporal and melodic metrics:
- **Pacing Speed:** Standard narration runs at 130–150 WPM. Intimate cinematic voiceover runs at **80 – 95 WPM** ($2.8$ to $3.2$ syllables/second).
- **Pitch Contour ($F_0$ Curve):** Tracked in Hertz (Hz). E.g., Starts at $112\text{ Hz}$ on *'कुछ घर'*, rises slightly to $118\text{ Hz}$ on *'पत्थर'*, drops across the 0.9s pause to $96\text{ Hz}$ on *'अधूरे'*, and resolves into an unvoiced $90\text{ Hz}$ vocal fry on *'होते हैं'*.
- **Word Stress Weights:** Decibel offset per keyword (e.g., `ईंट` receives $+2\text{ dB}$ crisp dental attack).
