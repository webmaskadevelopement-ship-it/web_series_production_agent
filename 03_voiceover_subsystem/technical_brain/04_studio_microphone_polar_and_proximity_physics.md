# STUDIO MICROPHONE POLAR & PROXIMITY PHYSICS (`technical_brain/04_studio_microphone_polar_and_proximity_physics.md`)

## 1. MICROPHONE RIGGING SPECIFICATION
To capture cinematic vocal warmth without plosive distortion:
- **Primary Microphone:** Neumann U87 Ai Large-Diaphragm Condenser set to Cardioid polar pattern.
- **Backup / Boom Rig:** Schoeps CMC641 supercardioid boom at 45cm for ambient spatial matching.
- **Distance & Angle:** Exactly $14\text{ cm}$ from lip plane, positioned **$15^\circ$ off-axis horizontally**.
  - *Acoustic Justification:* Prevents high-velocity air bursts from Devanagari labial aspirates (`प`, `फ`, `ब`, `भ`) from striking the gold-sputtered capsule directly, while retaining high transient air ($10\text{ kHz}-16\text{ kHz}$).
- **Pop Filtration:** Dual-layer Stedman Proscreen XL metal mesh filter positioned $5\text{ cm}$ from capsule.
- **Acoustic Environment:** Dead ADR vocal booth (Noise Criteria NC-15, RT60 $0.14\text{ seconds}$ at $500\text{ Hz}$).
