# LIVED-IN ENTROPY & ENVIRONMENTAL HISTORY (`creative_brain/03_lived_in_entropy_and_environmental_history.md`)

## 1. STRATIFIED GENERATIONAL WEAR
Sets must never look freshly painted or art-directed; they must show layers of physical time:
- **Rising Damp & Efflorescence:** Mineral salt crystals leaching through peeling pale indigo distemper up to $1.2\text{m}$ height, exposing underlying Lakhori brickwork.
- **Soot Accumulation:** Black greasy wood-smoke carbon deposits coating the ceiling above the southeast kitchen alcove.
- **Religious Entropy:** Wall niches (*Taak*) stained with dried vermilion (*Sindoor*), melted mustard-oil wax, and a dried string of mango leaves (*Toran*) hung two years prior.
