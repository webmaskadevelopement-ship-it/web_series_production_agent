# INDIAN VERNACULAR & REGIONAL ARCHITECTURE (`creative_brain/02_indian_vernacular_and_regional_architecture.md`)

## 1. THE BANARAS COURTYARD HAVELI TYPOLOGY
Sub-System 09 models authentic regional construction:
- **Spatial Typology:** Built c. 1895, the Sharma Haveli reflects the late Banarasi-Mughal merchant courtyard typology.
- **Central Aangan (Courtyard Well):** $8.0\text{m} \times 8.0\text{m}$ open-to-sky square sunken by $15\text{cm}$ stone curb (*Katghara*), drained by a hand-carved stone gargoyle drain (*Parnaala*) in the Northeast corner.
- **Perimeter Colonnade (*Dalaan*):** $2.4\text{m}$ deep veranda on all four sides supported by 12 fluted Chunar sandstone pillars with cusped Mughal arches.
- **Upper Mezzanine (*Jharokha*):** Second-story balconies with stone Jali lattice screens, allowing unseen surveillance of the courtyard below.
