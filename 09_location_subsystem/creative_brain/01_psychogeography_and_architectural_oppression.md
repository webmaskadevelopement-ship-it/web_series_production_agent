# PSYCHOGEOGRAPHY & ARCHITECTURAL OPPRESSION (`creative_brain/01_psychogeography_and_architectural_oppression.md`)

## 1. SPACE AS A MIRROR OF TRAUMA
In cinematic psychogeography, architecture is an extension of internal conflict:
- **Vertical Oppression:** High stone arches ($7.6\text{m}$ ceiling height) with dark upper vaults dwarf the characters, visually asserting the crushing permanence of ancestral lineage over modern transient ambition.
- **The Threshold Baffle (*Deorhi*):** Traditional havelis utilize an L-shaped privacy baffle at the main entrance, preventing a direct view from the street. When Raghav crosses this threshold, he is physically and visually severed from the modern exterior world.
