## 1. SCALE DIMENSIONS (METRIC COORDINATES)
Every location blueprint must establish bounding dimensions:
- **Width (X-axis):** $12.8\text{ meters}$ (East-West axis).
- **Length (Z-axis):** $14.2\text{ meters}$ (North-South axis).
- **Height (Y-axis):** $7.6\text{ meters}$ (Two-story interior facade).
- **Wall Thickness:** $60\text{ cm}$ solid load-bearing Lakhori brick and lime mortar.
- **Pillar Metrics:** 12 fluted pillars, $32\text{ cm}$ diameter, $2.8\text{ m}$ inter-columnar spacing, supporting $2.4\text{ m}$ deep veranda overhangs.
- **Doorway (Deorhi):** $1.35\text{ m}$ wide $\times 2.20\text{ m}$ high heavy double-leaf Burma teak doors.
