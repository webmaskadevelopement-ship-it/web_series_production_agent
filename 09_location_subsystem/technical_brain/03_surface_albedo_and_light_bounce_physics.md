| **Colonnade Walls** | Aged lime-surkhi plaster over Lakhori brick | `0.42` | `0.50` | Muted chalky white with pale indigo tint |
| **Veranda Ceilings** | Dark Burma teak timber beams | `0.12` | `0.18` | Deep warm umber shadow absorption |
| **Sandstone Pillars** | Hand-fluted Chunar stone with green moss | `0.24` | `0.58` (along wet moss grooves) | Olive-green specular kick |
