## 1. OPTICAL DEPTH THROWS & FOREGROUND OCCLUSION
The architectural layout is engineered to support deep telephoto lens compression:
- **Longest Diagonal Throw:** $14.2\text{ meters}$ diagonal sightline from the Northwest main threshold through the central pillar gap into the Southeast kitchen alcove (enables up to $135\text{mm}$ telephoto lens compression on interior dialogue).
- **Dirty-Frame Foreground Elements:** 12 fluted pillars, stone Jali screens, and hanging brass bell chains provide natural foreground occlusion at any $360^\circ$ pan angle, allowing the camera to shoot "past" architectural obstacles to emphasize surveillance and entrapment.
