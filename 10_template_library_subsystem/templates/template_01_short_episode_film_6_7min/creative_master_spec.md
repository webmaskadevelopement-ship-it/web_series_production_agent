# TEMPLATE 01: CREATIVE MASTER SPECIFICATION (`creative_master_spec.md`)

## 1. PURPOSE OF CREATIVE MASTER DELIVERABLES
The Creative Master Suite provides the **Executive / Client Shareable Edition** of the 6–7 minute episode film. It is engineered specifically for film investors, executive producers, cast, and creative collaborators who need to evaluate narrative, dialogue, emotional rhythm, and shot cadence without technical code blocks or optical mathematics.

---

## 2. DEPARTMENTAL CREATIVE MASTER SPECIFICATIONS

### 1. Master Screenplay (`01_Script_Creative_Master.md` / `.pdf`)
- **Length Constraint:** Strictly **6 to 7 pages** (industry-standard 1 page $\approx$ 1 minute).
- **Format:** Pristine Hollywood / Hindi Cinema bilingual format (Devanagari dialogue + Roman phonetics + English scene action).
- **Prose Purity:** Scene descriptions focus entirely on physical actor behavior, subtext, tension, and tangible environment.
- **Omissions:** **NO** camera angles, focal lengths, lighting kelvin, or audio frequency references in the action text.

---

### 2. Master Shot Breakdown (`02_Shot_Breakdown_Creative_Master.md` / `.pdf`)
- **Format:** **10-Column Master Production Tables ONLY** (grouped by Act, then by Scene).
- **Shot Coverage:** All **150 to 189 shots** are present row by row. Zero shots skipped.
- **Table Schema:**
  | Shot ID | Shot Type | Camera Angle | Camera Movement | Lens Focal Length | Subject & Dramatic Action | Sound / Dialogue Cue | Lighting Atmosphere | Sec | Dramatic Function |
  | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
- **Omissions:** **COMPLETELY OMITS** the Deep Technical Decompilation JSON blocks (no Cooke /i distortion profiles, no sensor dynamic range curves, no FACS Action Units, no PBR roughness tables).
- **Result:** An elegant, readable 8–10 page tabular overview perfect for production meetings and client review.

---

### 3. Voiceover Deliverable (`03_Voiceover_Creative_Master.md` / `.pdf`)
- **Format:** **Plain Prose Voiceover Script**.
- **Content:** The complete literary narrator monologue/dialogue formatted as rich, performable prose with scene markers and pacing notes.
- **Readability:** Intended for the voice actor or director to hold in a booth or table-read.
- **Omissions:** **NO** DSP chains, EQ Hz cuts/boosts, preamp impedances, microphone distance physics, or IPA phonetic transcriptions.

---

### 4. Storyboarding Pre-Vis (`04_Storyboarding_Creative_Master.md`)
- **Format:** Clean 2-liner narrative descriptions per shot with emotional beat and key visual framing.
- **Omissions:** Excludes mathematical dynamic symmetry vectors and GenAI prompt tokens.

---
