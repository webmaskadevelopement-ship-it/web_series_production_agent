# SLUGLINE & SUB-LOCATION GEOGRAPHY (`technical_brain/01_slugline_and_sub_location_geography.md`)

## 1. INDUSTRY SLUGLINE SYNTAX
Screenplay scene headers (sluglines) provide the critical operational metadata required by assistant directors, location managers, and cinematographers. Every slugline in `01_Script.md` must follow this standardized syntax:

```text
[ENVIRONMENT PREFIX]. [MASTER LOCATION] - [SUB-LOCATION ZONE] - [TIME / SOLAR STATE] [LIGHTING CONTEXT]
```

### Examples
- `INT. SHARMA ANCESTRAL HAVELI - CENTRAL COURTYARD (VARANASI) - DUSK (18:15 IST - BLUE HOUR / TUNGSTEN)`
- `EXT. AHILYABAI GHAT - INNER COBBLESTONE ALLEY - NIGHT (21:30 IST - MONSOON RAIN)`
- `INT./EXT. MAIN THRESHOLD (DEORHI) - DUSK (TRANSITIONING TO NIGHT)`

---

## 2. METADATA ANCHORS
Every slugline in `01_Script.md` must link directly to:
1. `location_uuid`: E.g., `LOC_01` (Sharma Haveli).
2. `solar_elevation`: E.g., `-2.5° (Blue hour zenith)`.
3. `atmospheric_condition`: E.g., `Overcast monsoon drizzle / 88% humidity`.
