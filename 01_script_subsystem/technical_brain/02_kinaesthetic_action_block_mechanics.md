# KINAESTHETIC ACTION BLOCK MECHANICS (`technical_brain/02_kinaesthetic_action_block_mechanics.md`)

## 1. PRESENT-TENSE CAMERA-READY PROSE
Screenplay action blocks must describe only what can be **seen by the camera lens** and **heard by the microphone**. Internal character monologues, literary backstory, and non-photographable psychological summaries are strictly banned.

### Prohibited vs. Mandatory Examples
- **FORBIDDEN (Unfilmable Literary Prose):**
  *Raghav stands at the door feeling the crushing burden of having neglected his ailing father for six years. He remembers the cold Delhi courtroom and hates himself for choosing his ambition over his roots.*
- **MANDATORY (Kinaesthetic Camera-Ready Action):**
  *Raghav (CHAR_01) freezes on the raised stone threshold. His knuckles whiten around the wooden handle of his dripping umbrella (PROP_04). Water dribbles down his linen collar, but he does not wipe it away. His gaze fixates on the empty woven cot in the corner of the veranda.*

---

## 2. BLOCKING COORDINATES IN PROSE
Action blocks must embed spatial vectors that correspond directly with `02_shot_breakdown_subsystem` and `07_character_subsystem`:
- Relative positions: `Left quadrant`, `At central fluted pillar`, `3 meters upstage`.
- Prop interactions: Always tag hero props with their persistent identifiers `[PROP_XX]`.
