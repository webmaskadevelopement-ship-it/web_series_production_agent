# CHARACTER SPEECH REGISTER MATRIX (`matrices/character_speech_register_matrix.md`)

## 1. SPEECH STRATIFICATION IN HINDI CINEMA

| Speech Register | Primary Social Milieu | Linguistic Markers & Vocabulary | Character Representation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tatsama / Sanskritized Hindi** | Traditional academic, priestly, or philosophical authority | High frequency of classical loanwords: स्मृति (Memory), मर्यादा (Honor), पूर्वज (Ancestors), दायित्व (Duty). | Traditional elders, scholars, formal recitations. |
| **Tadbhava / Vernacular Hindi** | Domestic household, agrarian, or small-town North India | Evolved vernacular forms: आँगन (Courtyard), दीया (Lamp), खटिया (Cot), बियाह (Wedding). | Savitri, local shopkeepers, domestic caretakers. |
| **Urdu-Inflected Hindustani** | Cultural, poetic, and emotional intimacy in UP / Old Delhi | Persian/Arabic roots with Nukta: काग़ज़ (Paper), वजूद (Existence), ख़ामोशी (Silence), फ़ैसला (Verdict). | Poetic exchanges, retrospective narrators. |
| **Metropolitan Hinglish** | Corporate, legal, and urban bureaucratic elite | Code-mixing: "valuation clear है", "registry सोमवार को होगी", "settlement deed". | Raghav Sharma (in public / lawyer persona). |
