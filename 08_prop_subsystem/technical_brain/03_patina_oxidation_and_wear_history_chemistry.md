# PATINA, OXIDATION & WEAR HISTORY CHEMISTRY (`technical_brain/03_patina_oxidation_and_wear_history_chemistry.md`)

## 1. AGING CHEMISTRY OF PROPS
Props must carry observable chemical history:
- **Copper Carbonate Verdigris ($\text{Cu}_2\text{CO}_3(\text{OH})_2$):** Pale green powdery crust inside brass engravings, produced by decades of contact with moisture and airborne carbon dioxide.
- **Slaked-Lime Crust ($\text{Ca(OH)}_2$):** Chalky off-white/red residue deposited inside betel box hinges.
- **Micro-Scratch Topology:** Mirror-burnished thumbwear on the lid handle, contrasting with darkened, unpolished recessed walls.
