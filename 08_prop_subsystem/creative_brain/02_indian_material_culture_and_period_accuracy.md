# INDIAN MATERIAL CULTURE & PERIOD ACCURACY (`creative_brain/02_indian_material_culture_and_period_accuracy.md`)

## 1. AUTHENTIC REGIONAL DOMESTIC MATERIAL CULTURE
Sub-System 08 rejects generic contemporary Indian set dressing in favor of hyper-authentic regional artifacts:
- **Moradabad Hand-Engraved Yellow Brass (`Chikan` floral relief):** Hand-beaten brassware with verdigris deposits in recessed crevices.
- **Katha & Chuna Stains:** Red slaked-lime and catechu crust along the hinge of betel boxes, accumulated over 40 years of continuous use.
- **Yellowed Indian Post Money Order Slips:** Carbon-paper forms with faded purple rubber stamps from the Dashashwamedh Post Office.
