# CHEKHOV'S GUN & NARRATIVE OBJECT SYMBOLISM (`creative_brain/01_chekhovs_gun_and_narrative_object_symbolism.md`)

## 1. OBJECTS AS EMOTIONAL STORAGE CONTAINERS
In cinema, physical objects are not inert props; they are emotional batteries charged with narrative history:
- **The Father's Brass Paan-Daan (`PROP_01`):** Represents the deceased father's daily domestic ritual and moral authority. When opened, it reveals not betel leaves, but 14 uncashed money orders sent by Raghav over 6 years—turning the domestic box into an indictment of filial betrayal.
- **The Modern Black Umbrella (`PROP_04`):** A sleek synthetic corporate artifact dripping water onto ancestral stone; symbolizes urban intrusion into sacred space.
