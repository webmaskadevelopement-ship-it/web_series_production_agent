## 2. METADATA RECORDING REQUIREMENTS
Every shot in `02_Shot_Breakdown.md` must record Cooke `/i` protocol metadata:
- Lens Series & Focal Length (mm)
- Shooting T-Stop (e.g., `T2.8` — closed down 1/3 stop for sweet-spot sharpness)
- Minimum Focus Distance vs. Actual Sensor-to-Subject Focus Distance (m)
- Image Circle Coverage (mm)
