# LIGHTING RATIOS, KELVIN & IRE ZONES (`technical_brain/06_lighting_ratios_kelvin_and_ire_zones.md`)

## 1. PHOTOMETRIC EXPOSURE ZONES (IRE)
Every shot breakdown in `02_Shot_Breakdown.md` must calibrate exposure against standard IRE waveform values on the ARRI LogC4 / Rec.709 scale:

| IRE Value | Zone & Exposure Level | Target Screen Elements |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **94 – 98 IRE** | Specular Highlight Ceiling | Practical oil flame core, direct tungsten filament, specular wet stone reflections. |
| **70 – 78 IRE** | High Diffuse Reflection | White linen shirts, paper deeds, direct sky overcast zenith. |
| **40 – 45 IRE** | Key Skin Tone (Indian Melanin Type IV-V)| Lit cheek of Savitri / Raghav (-0.5 to -1.0 EV below middle grey for mood). |
| **18 – 24 IRE** | 18% Middle Grey / Ambient Midtones | Weathered pink-ochre Chunar sandstone courtyard floor. |
| **8 – 14 IRE** | Shadow Cheek / Ambient Fill | Shadow side of actor's face in 8:1 chiaroscuro contrast. |
| **4 – 6 IRE** | Deep Shadow Texture Floor | Deep corridor stone arches (retains texture without digital clipping). |
| **0 – 3 IRE** | Absolute Optical Black | Unlit recesses, extreme negative fill boundaries. |

## 2. COLOR TEMPERATURE CONTRAST
Scenes in Sub-System 02 frequently deploy **Chromatically Opposed Sources**:
- **Ambient Zenith Fill:** `6800K – 7200K` (Natural blue hour twilight overcast sky, CRI 99).
- **Key Practical Source:** `2100K – 2200K` (Mustard-oil diya + concealed Astera NYX bulb dimmed to 12%, TLCI 98).
- **Background Accent:** `2700K` (Flickering 40W tungsten bulb in rusted corridor wire cage).
