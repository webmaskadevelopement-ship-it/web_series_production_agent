# OPTICAL ABERRATIONS, DISTORTION & FLARES (`technical_brain/05_optical_aberrations_distortion_and_flares.md`)

## 1. OPTICAL ARTIFACT CODIFICATION
No real-world cinema lens is mathematically sterile. The organic tactile character of cinematic imagery comes from controlled optical imperfections:

| Artifact | Measured Parameter | Typical Range | Visual Characteristic |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Barrel / Pincushion Distortion** | Float offset (0.0 to 1.0) | `0.02 – 0.05` | Gentle outward bowing of vertical stone doorframes on wide anamorphic lenses. |
| **Edge Softness** | Peripheral resolution loss | `0.04 – 0.08` | Falloff of micro-contrast in the outer 15% of frame, drawing eye to center. |
| **Veiling Glare** | Contrast reduction | `0.03 – 0.06` | Gentle milkiness in dark areas when practical flame enters perimeter of frame. |
| **Chromatic Aberration** | Color fringe offset | `0.02 – 0.04` | Subtle cyan-magenta fringe around high-contrast courtyard arch openings. |
| **Focus Breathing** | Field-of-view shift | `2% – 5%` | Slight focal length expansion/contraction during Preston follow-focus racks. |
| **Horizontal Anamorphic Flaring** | Streak color and threshold | Amber / Blue | Horizontal streak triggered by practical oil flame or bare incandescent filaments. |
