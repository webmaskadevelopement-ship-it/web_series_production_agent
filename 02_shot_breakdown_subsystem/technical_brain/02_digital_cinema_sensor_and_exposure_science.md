# DIGITAL CINEMA SENSOR & EXPOSURE SCIENCE (`technical_brain/02_digital_cinema_sensor_and_exposure_science.md`)

## 1. CINEMA SENSOR SPECIFICATIONS

### ARRI Alexa 35 (ALEV 4 CMOS Sensor)
- **Active Sensor Area:** 4608 x 3164 pixels (Open Gate 4:3 / 16:9).
- **Dynamic Range:** 17.0 stops (9.2 stops above 18% middle grey, 7.8 stops below middle grey).
- **Base Sensitivity:** EI 800 (Enhanced Sensitivity Mode disabled for lowest noise floor).
- **Color Science:** REVEAL Color Science / ARRI Wide Gamut 4 (AWG4) / LogC4.
- **Exposure Response:** Logarithmic shoulder rolloff protecting highlights up to 98 IRE without clipping; shadow compression retains porous stone textures down to 4 IRE.

### Sony Venice 2 (8.6K Full-Frame CMOS)
- **Base Dual ISO:** EI 800 / EI 3200.
- **Dynamic Range:** 16.0 stops.
- **Color Science:** S-Gamut3.Cine / S-Log3.

## 2. SHUTTER ANGLE & EXPOSURE TIMING
- **Standard Cadence:** $180.0^\circ$ shutter angle at $24.000\text{ fps}$ (exposure time: $1/48\text{ second}$).
- **Staccato / Agitated Cadence:** $90.0^\circ$ or $45.0^\circ$ shutter ($1/96\text{s}$ or $1/192\text{s}$) for rapid combat or panicked violence.
- **Dreamy / Temporal Smear Cadence:** $270.0^\circ$ to $356.0^\circ$ shutter ($1/32\text{s}$ or $1/24\text{s}$) for fever states or grief disassociation.
