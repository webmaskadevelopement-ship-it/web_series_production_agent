# FINCHER & PARK CHAN-WOOK MOTIVATED CAMERA METHODOLOGY (`creative_brain/01_fincher_park_chanwook_camera_motivation.md`)

## 1. THE LAW OF MATHEMATICAL CAMERA MOTIVATION
In the cinematic philosophies of David Fincher (*Zodiac*, *Mindhunter*) and Park Chan-wook (*Decision to Leave*, *Oldboy*), the camera is an omnipresent, hyper-disciplined psychological observer. **Every camera movement must be mathematically justified by character psychology.**

Unmotivated handheld shake, wandering zoom moves, and casual whip-pans are strictly forbidden in this system unless representing complete character psychotic breakdown.

---

## 2. CAMERA MOVEMENT TAXONOMY

1. **The Inevitable Linear Creep (Z-axis Dolly Push):**
   - Slow, constant velocity tracking push (e.g., $8.0\text{ cm/s}$) along a geometric axis of symmetry.
   - Used to simulate inescapable moral reckoning, narrowing options, or impending confrontation.
   - Imperfection score: `0.0` (mounted on steel track over vibration pads).
2. **The Clinical Pan / Tilt (Rotational Lock):**
   - The camera pans only when an actor's eye-line or physical displacement leads the frame by exactly 4 to 8 frames.
   - Stops with zero bounce-back on the fluid head (OConnor 2575D drag setting 4/10).
3. **Park Chan-wook Split-Frame Architectural Occlusion:**
   - Using physical foreground architecture (stone pillars, door jambs, metal window grills) to physically slice the widescreen frame in half.
   - Isolates two characters spatially even when they share the same physical room, visually embodying irreconcilable moral worldviews.
