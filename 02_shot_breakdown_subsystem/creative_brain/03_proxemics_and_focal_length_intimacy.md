# PROXEMICS & FOCAL LENGTH INTIMACY (`creative_brain/03_proxemics_and_focal_length_intimacy.md`)

## 1. THE PSYCHOLOGY OF OPTICAL PERSPECTIVE
A camera lens is not merely a tool for magnification; it is an emotional transducer that dictates the viewer's psychological intimacy with the character. 

Selecting a focal length requires analyzing the character's social proxemic zone (Edward T. Hall's Proxemic Theory):
- **Intimate Distance (0 – 0.5m):** 85mm–100mm Macro Close-Up. The viewer enters the actor's breathing and tactile sphere.
- **Personal Distance (0.5m – 1.2m):** 50mm Prime at T2.0–T2.8. Natural human field of view; honest, unvarnished emotional presence.
- **Social Distance (1.2m – 3.5m):** 35mm–40mm Medium Shot. Frames the character within their immediate social conflict and spatial obstacles.
- **Public / Architectural Distance (3.5m+):** 21mm–28mm Extreme Wide Master. The character is reduced to an insignificant figure dwarfed by generational architecture or moral guilt.

## 2. ANAMORPHIC COMPRESSION DYNAMICS
When deploying Anamorphic Primes (e.g., Cooke Anamorphic /i 2x):
- A 50mm Anamorphic provides the horizontal field of view of a 25mm spherical lens, while retaining the shallow depth-of-field and vertical compression of a 50mm.
- This creates simultaneous spatial context (wide environment) and intense psychological isolation (shallow subject separation).
