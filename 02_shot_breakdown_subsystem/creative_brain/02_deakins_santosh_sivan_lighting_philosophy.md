# DEAKINS & SANTOSH SIVAN LIGHTING PHILOSOPHY (`creative_brain/02_deakins_santosh_sivan_lighting_philosophy.md`)

## 1. MOTIVATED NATURALISM VS. EXPRESSIVE CHIAROSCURO
The lighting philosophy of Sub-System 02 synthesizes Roger Deakins’ (*Sicario*, *Blade Runner 2049*) doctrine of **strictly motivated natural sources** with Santosh Sivan’s (*Dil Se..*, *Asoka*, *The Terrorist*) masterclass in **tactile Indian chiaroscuro, atmospheric wet-downs, and warm flame dynamics**.

---

## 2. THE THREE CORE TENETS

1. **Every Light Must Have a Physical Reason to Exist:**
   - Unmotivated "movie lighting" (e.g., backlight on an actor in a dark corridor with no window or lamp) is forbidden.
   - All light must originate from visible practicals: twilight sky zenith through courtyard roof, oil diya, kerosene lantern, or single caged incandescent bulb.
2. **Negative Fill as the Primary Sculpting Tool:**
   - Great cinematography is defined not by where light is placed, but by where it is subtracted.
   - Large black solids (12x12 Ultra-Bounce Black) must be rigged to swallow ambient bounce on the shadow side of faces, sustaining steep 8:1 to 12:1 contrast ratios.
3. **Specular Wet-Downs & Atmospheric Particulates:**
   - Indian sandstone, brick, and skin sheen in high humidity come alive under specular glancing angles. The lighting plot must exploit water reflection angles off porous stone to create depth planes.
