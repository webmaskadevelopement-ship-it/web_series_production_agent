# CHROMATIC PSYCHOLOGY & RASA PALETTES (`creative_brain/01_chromatic_psychology_and_rasa_palettes.md`)

## 1. EMOTIONAL COLOR THEORY IN INDIAN CINEMA
Color grading is not a cosmetic final polish; it is a subconscious psychological narrative track. Sub-System 05 maps narrative emotion (*Rasa*) directly to split-complementary color palettes:
- **Karuna (Pathos / Ancestral Grief):** Cold slate-cyan shadows (`#1B262C`) paired with bruised amber-gold highlights (`#D9822B`). The coldness of the stone courtyard embodies emotional detachment, while the warm practical diya represents fragile human memory.
- **Raudra (Suppressed Fury):** Deep terracotta-red saturation (`#8C2D19`) clashing with sickly olive-ochre midtones.
- **Shanta (Acceptance / Catharsis):** Monochromatic warm platinum and pale ochre with neutral, uncast black levels.
