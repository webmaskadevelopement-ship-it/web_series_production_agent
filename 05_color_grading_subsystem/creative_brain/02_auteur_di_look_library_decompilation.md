# AUTEUR DI LOOK LIBRARY DECOMPILATION (`creative_brain/02_auteur_di_look_library_decompilation.md`)

## 1. CINEMATIC LOOK BENCHMARKS

1. **Roger Deakins / Stefan Sonnenfeld (Company 3):**
   - Naturalistic contrast, rich tonal roll-off, unclipped organic shadows.
   - Preserves natural skin-tone colorimetry while allowing motivated sources (fire, tungsten, neon) to push bold saturation without digital clipping.
2. **Christopher Doyle (Wong Kar-wai - *In the Mood for Love*):**
   - Deep amber, emerald green, and tungsten-soaked night palettes with heavy subtractive density.
   - Saturated colors are darkened rather than brightened, creating rich velvet textures.
3. **Darius Khondji (*Se7en*, *Uncut Gems*):**
   - Bleach bypass emulation (ENR process), silver retention, stark desaturated midtones, and inky, menacing shadows.
