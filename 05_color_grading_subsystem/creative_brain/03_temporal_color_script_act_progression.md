# TEMPORAL COLOR SCRIPT & ACT PROGRESSION (`creative_brain/03_temporal_color_script_act_progression.md`)

## 1. THREE-ACT COLOR GRADING EVOLUTION
The color grade must evolve dynamically across the three acts of the film:

- **Act 1 (The Threshold of Return):**
  - Contrast Pivot: `0.385` (slightly below middle grey to protect sandstone shadow texture).
  - Global Saturation: `-8.0`.
  - Palette: Chiaroscuro twilight blue vs. intimate diya amber.
- **Act 2 (The Trial of Memory):**
  - Contrast Pivot: Drops to `0.320`.
  - Global Saturation: Pulled down to `-15.0`, while yellow/amber hues from the diya and memory scenes are boosted to $+24.0$.
  - Palette: Claustrophobic gloom, muddy shadows, stark specular highlights.
- **Act 3 (The Crucible of Severance):**
  - Contrast Pivot: Rises to `0.450`.
  - Dawn daylight introduces clean, cool platinum whites (`5200K`), washing out the warm nighttime amber and leaving cold, clear truth.
