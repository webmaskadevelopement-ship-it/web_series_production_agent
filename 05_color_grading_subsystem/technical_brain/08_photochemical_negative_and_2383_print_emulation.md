# PHOTOCHEMICAL NEGATIVE & KODAK 2383 PRINT EMULATION (`technical_brain/08_photochemical_negative_and_2383_print_emulation.md`)

## 1. TWO-STAGE PHOTOCHEMICAL EMULATION
Color reproduction in modern cinematic grading emulates two distinct photochemical stages:

1. **Negative Film Response (Kodak Vision3 500T 5219):**
   - High dynamic range, soft logarithmic highlight shoulder, and organic blue-green shadow sensitivity.
2. **Positive Print Film Response (Kodak 2383 Color Print Film):**
   - Steep contrast S-curve, high color separation, subtractive yellow-amber highlight rolloff, and deep cyan-biased shadow toe.
   - **White Point Calibration:** D60 ($6000\text{K}$) curve calibrated within DaVinci Wide Gamut for natural theatrical warmth.
