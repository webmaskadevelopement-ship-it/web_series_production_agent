# HSL 3D QUALIFIER & SKIN-TONE PROTECTION (`technical_brain/06_hsl_3d_qualifier_and_skin_tone_protection.md`)

## 1. THE VECTORSCOPE I-LINE LOCK
Indian skin tones (Fitzpatrick Types IV and V) contain rich melanin undertones that are easily contaminated by aggressive teal/cyan shadow split-toning.

Node 08 deploys a 3D HSL qualifier isolating skin:
- **Hue Center:** Locked precisely along the **Vectorscope I-Line** ($10:30$ o'clock position on the scope).
- **Hue Shift:** $+1.5^\circ$ toward yellow-olive.
- **Saturation Offset:** $+8.0$ (restoring organic blood warmth).
- **Luminance Offset:** $-4.0$ (deepening melanin density without specular blowout).
- **Key Feathering:** Clean soft falloff preventing harsh edges around hairlines, temples, and jawlines.
