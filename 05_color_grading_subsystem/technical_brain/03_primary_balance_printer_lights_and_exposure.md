# PRIMARY BALANCE, PRINTER LIGHTS & EXPOSURE (`technical_brain/03_primary_balance_printer_lights_and_exposure.md`)

## 1. PHOTOCHEMICAL PRINTER LIGHT OFFSET MATH
In high-end film color grading, primary balancing is conducted using **Printer Lights** (calibrated in quarter-point increments from $0$ to $50$ points, centered at $25-25-25$):

- **1 Full Printer Light Point:** Equals approximately $1/6\text{ stop}$ ($0.166\text{ EV}$) exposure change.
- **Quarter-Point Increment ($0.25$ pts):** Micro-adjusts color temperature and tint without damaging highlight roll-off.
- **Master Exposure Offset:** Applied in Exposure Value (EV). E.g., $-0.35\text{ EV}$ underexposure preserves practical flame detail up to $98\text{ IRE}$ and deepens architectural negative fill.
