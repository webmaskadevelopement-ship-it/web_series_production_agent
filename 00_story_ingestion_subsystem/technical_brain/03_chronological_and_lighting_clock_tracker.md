# CHRONOLOGICAL & LIGHTING CLOCK TRACKER (`technical_brain/03_chronological_and_lighting_clock_tracker.md`)

## 1. TEMPORAL & SOLAR CONTINUITY
Cinema is a temporal art where atmospheric conditions and lighting states establish narrative rhythm and psychological pressure. Inconsistencies between scene time-of-day, sun angle, and practical lighting undermine cinematic realism.

The Ingestor tracks the story's chronological clock, solar geometry, and weather transitions across all scenes.

---

## 2. THE 7 SOLAR/LIGHTING STATES

| Lighting State Code | Real-World Time (IST) | Sun Elevation & Azimuth | Correlated Color Temp | Dramatic Context |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `DAWN_BHOR` | 05:15 – 06:30 IST | -2° to +12° (East) | 3800K – 4500K (Soft lavender-peach) | New beginnings, departure, fragile clarity. |
| `MORNING_DOPAHR_PRE`| 07:00 – 10:30 IST | +15° to +48° (East-SE) | 5200K – 5600K (Clean directional light) | Domestic awakening, arrival, bustling streets. |
| `HARSH_NOON` | 11:30 – 14:00 IST | +65° to +88° (Zenith) | 5800K – 6500K (Deep downward shadows) | Exposure, blistering heat, relentless pressure. |
| `AFTERNOON_DOPAHR` | 14:30 – 16:30 IST | +45° to +25° (West-SW) | 5000K – 4500K (Slanted amber light) | Heavy languor, exhaustion, lingering decay. |
| `GOLDEN_HOUR_SANDHYA`| 17:00 – 18:00 IST | +12° to 0° (West horizon)| 2800K – 3200K (Rich golden-orange) | Nostalgia, poetic transience, longing. |
| `BLUE_HOUR_TWILIGHT`| 18:15 – 19:00 IST | -1° to -8° (Zenith sky) | 6500K – 7500K (Deep cobalt/slate) | The threshold of secrecy, rising coldness. |
| `NIGHT_RAAT` | 19:30 – 04:30 IST | Below horizon | Dominated by practicals (1900K-2800K) | Intimacy, confrontation, shadow entrapment. |

## 3. WEATHER & ATMOSPHERIC CONTINUITY MATRIX
The Ingestor models continuous environmental factors across scene transitions:
- **Relative Humidity (%):** High humidity (80–90% during monsoon) dictates wet-down specular reflections on sandstone, skin sweat sheen, and acoustic high-frequency absorption.
- **Precipitation Stages:** Overcast build-up $\rightarrow$ torrential downpour $\rightarrow$ steady dripping drizzle $\rightarrow$ post-monsoon steamy mist.
- **Wind & Draft:** Still suffocating air vs. sudden gusts through courtyard cusped arches rattling wooden shutters.
