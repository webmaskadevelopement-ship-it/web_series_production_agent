# HINDI DIALECT & REGIONAL GEOGRAPHY MATRIX (`matrices/hindi_dialect_geography_matrix.md`)

## 1. REGIONAL PHONETIC & LEXICAL PROFILES

| Dialect Zone | Geography & Cultural Epicenter | Phonetic Markers & Consonant Shifts | Archaic / Colloquial Vocabulary | Dramatic Register |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Khari Boli (Standard)** | Delhi, Western UP, Haryana border | Crisp dental/retroflex contrast, neutral vowel duration, Persian/Urdu loan words. | कानून (Law), फैसला (Decision), मजबूरी (Helplessness), इमारत (Building). | Urban legalism, bureaucratic detachment, modern polish. |
| **Awadhi** | Lucknow, Ayodhya, Faizabad, Rae Bareli | Softened dental consonants, prolonged final vowels, gentle cadence. | अम्मा (Mother), बियाह (Marriage), पुरवा (East wind), संझा (Dusk). | Poetic nostalgia, feudal elegance, understated sorrow. |
| **Bhojpuri / Purvanchali**| Varanasi, Gorakhpur, Ballia, Bhojpur | Dropping of formal pronouns; substitution of 'हम' for 'मैं'; strong aspirated stops; retroflex flap 'ड़'. | तसला (Basin), आँगन (Courtyard), बाबू (Son/Clerk), नाही (No), कागद (Paper). | Raw emotional directness, domestic permanence, deep grievance. |
| **Braj Bhasha** | Mathura, Agra, Aligarh, Bharatpur | Broad open vowels ('औ', 'ऐ'), rounded 'ल', lyrical singing cadence. | मोरा (Mine), तोरा (Yours), बटोही (Traveler), छाँह (Shade). | Folk lyricism, devotional tenderness, rustic humility. |
| **Bundeli** | Jhansi, Gwalior, Sagar, Orchha | Hard dental stops, guttural contraction, dropped aspirates. | हमाओ (Ours), तुमाओ (Yours), काय को (Why), डोकरा (Old man). | Rugged martial defiance, stark rural pragmatism. |
| **Marwari / Rajasthani** | Jodhpur, Bikaner, Shekhawati | Elongated retroflex 'ण', vocalic shifts, honorific enclitic '-सा' (Sa). | हुकुम (Order), हवेली (Mansion), पधारो (Welcome), घाटो (Loss). | Aristocratic formality, masked financial anxiety, rigid etiquette. |

## 2. DIALECT CODE-SWITCHING PROTOCOL
When an ingested story features a character moving between worlds (e.g., Raghav Sharma: born in Varanasi Brahmin family, educated in Allahabad, practicing corporate law in Delhi):
- **Default State (Public):** Metropolitan Khari Boli infused with English corporate/legal vocabulary (`valuation`, `registry`, `affidavit`).
- **Triggered State (Under Maternal Pressure):** Syntax collapses back into Purvanchali/Awadhi phrasing and idioms (`"हमसे नाही हो पाई अम्मा"`).
